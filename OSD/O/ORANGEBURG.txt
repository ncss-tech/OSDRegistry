Mean Annual Precipitation: 1321 millimeters (52 inches)

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Fine-loamy, kaolinitic, thermic Typic Kandiudults

TYPICAL PEDON: Orangeburg loamy sand, in a cultivated field (Colors are for moist soil).

  Ap--0 to 18 centimeters (0 to 7 inches); dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) loamy sand; weak fine granular structure; very friable; many fine and medium roots; strongly acid; clear smooth boundary. (8 to 25 centimeters (3 to 10 inches) thick)

  BA--18 to 30 centimeters (7 to 12 inches); strong brown (7.5YR 5/6) sandy loam; weak fine subangular blocky structure; very friable; many fine roots; sand grains bridged and coated with clay; very strongly acid; clear smooth boundary. (0 to 30 centimeters (0 to 12 inches) thick)

  Bt1--30 to 137 centimeters (12 to 54 inches); yellowish red (5YR 4/6) sandy clay loam; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable; many fine roots; many fine pores; common distinct clay films on faces of peds; very strongly acid; gradual smooth boundary.

  Bt2--137 to 183 centimeters (54 to 72 inches); yellowish red (5YR 5/8) sandy clay loam; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable; few fine roots; few faint clay films on faces of peds; few fine distinct yellowish brown (10YR 5/6) mottles; very strongly acid. (Combined thickness of the Bt horizons is 132 to 178 centimeters (52 to 70 inches) or more)

TYPE LOCATION: Dougherty County, Georgia. Approximately 0.6 mile west on Antioch Road from the intersection with Gravel Hill Road and about 660 yards north in cultivated field. USGS Putney, Georgia topographic quadrangle. 

Latitude--31.4853
Longitude-- -84.0722
Datum--WGS84

RANGE IN CHARACTERISTICS: 
Solum thickness: 183 to 244 centimeters (72 to 96 inches) but ranges from 152 to 305 centimeters (60 to 120 inches) 
Depth to Seasonal High Water Table: Greater than 152 centimeters (60 inches)
Ironstone nodules: 0 to 10 percent, by volume, throughout the solum 
Soil reaction: Very strongly acid to moderately acid in the A and Bt1 horizons and very strongly acid to strongly acid in the Bt2 and underlying horizons.

RANGE OF INDIVIDUAL HORIZONS:
A or Ap horizon: 
Color--hue of 5YR to 10YR, value of 3 to 5, chroma of 2 to 6 
Texture--sand, loamy sand, loamy fine sand, sandy loam, fine sandy loam or sandy clay loam.

E horizon (if it occurs):
Color--hue of 5YR to 10YR, value of 4 to 6, chroma of 3 to 6 
Texture--sand, loamy sand or sandy loam

BA or BE horizon:
Color--hue of 2.5YR to 10YR, value of 4 to 6, chroma of 4 to 8 
Texture--sandy loam or fine sandy loam

Bt horizon (upper part):
Color--hue of 2.5YR or 5YR, value of 4 to 6, chroma of 6 or 8. Hues of 7.5YR are allowed within the upper 25 centimeters (10 inches). 
Texture--sandy clay loam. Clay content of the upper 51 centimeters (20 inches) of the Bt horizon ranges from 20 to 34 percent and silt content is less than 20 percent. Some pedons have a sandy loam or fine sandy loam horizon that is less than 18 centimeters (7 inches) thick.

Bt horizon (lower part):
Color--hue of 10R to 5YR, value of 4 to 6, chroma of 6 or 8. Mottles in shades of yellow and brown range from none to common. 
Texture--sandy clay loam, clay loam, or sandy clay with less than 45 percent clay.

The BC horizon (if it occurs):
Color--hue of 2.5YR to 7.5YR, value of 4 or 5, chroma of 6 or 8. Mottles in shades of brown, yellow, red or gray range from none to many. 
Texture--sandy loam, sandy clay loam, or sandy clay.

COMPETING SERIES: 
Norfolk soils--have yellower subsoils
Thursa soils--have yellower upper subsoils that are more than 25 centimeters (10 inches) thick

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Landscape: Coastal Plain
Landform: Uplands
Parent Material: Loamy and clayey marine sediments
Elevation: 52 to 152 meters (170 to 500 feet) above mean sea level
Frost Free Period: 215 to 270 days 
Mean Annual Air Temperature: 17 to 20 degrees C (63 to 68 degrees F)
Mean Annual Precipitation: 1067 to 1346 millimeters (42 to 53 inches)

Orangeburg soils are on nearly level to strongly sloping uplands of the Coastal Plain. Slopes range from 0 to 25 percent. Near the type location, the mean annual temperature ranges from 63 to 68 degrees F., and the mean annual precipitation ranges from 42 to 53 inches. The climate is humid subtropical. The number of frost-free days ranges from 215 to 270. Elevation ranges from 170 to 500 feet above sea level.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: 
Americus soils--are somewhat excessively drained, weakly expressed Bt horizons and are rhodic. 
Bama soils--have more than 20 percent silt in the control section. 
Benevolence soils--are coarse-loamy
Dothan soils--have horizons with more than 5 percent plinthite 
Eustis soils--are somewhat excessively drained and have a weakly expressed Bt horizon 
Faceville soils--are clayey 
Fuquay soils--have horizons with more than 5 percent plinthite and are arenic
Grady soils--are poorly drained, clayey, and occur in depressions or along shallow drainageways
Greenville soils--are clayey and rhodic
Lucy soils--are arenic
Noboco soils--have siliceous mineralogy and a yellower subsoil
Red Bay soils--are rhodic
Tifton soils--have horizons with more than 5 percent plinthite and ironstone
Vaucluse soils--have the upper boundary of a brittle layer within 36 inches of the soil surface, have a yellower subsoil, and occur on upland slope breaks
Wagram soils--are arenic and have a yellower subsoil

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: 
Agricultural Drainage Class: Well drained

USE AND VEGETATION: 
Major uses: Cropland, pasture, and woodland
Dominant vegetation: Where cultivated--cotton, corn, tobacco, and peanuts. Where wooded--longleaf pine, shortleaf pine, loblolly pine, various oaks, hickory, and dogwood.

Diagnostic horizons and features recognized in this pedon are:
Ochric epipedon--the zone from 0 to 18 centimeters (0 to 7 inches) (Ap horizon).
Argillic horizon--the zone from 18 to 183 centimeters (7 to 72 inches) (BA, Bt1, and Bt2 horizons).
Kandic horizon--the zone from 18 to 183 centimeters (7 to 72 inches) with low activity clay in most of the upper 102 centimeters (40 inches) (BA, Bt1 and Bt2 horizons).
