PRUCREE SERIES

The Prucree series consists of moderately deep, well drained soils formed in colluvium or slope alluvium over residuum weathered from sandstone, siltstone, or quartzite.  These soils are on ridges, mountain slopes, fan remnants, and hillsides.  Slopes are 4 to 30 percent.  Mean annual precipitation is about 400 mm, and mean annual air temperature is about 3.9 degrees C.

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy, mixed, superactive, frigid Pachic Haploxerolls

TYPICAL PEDON: Prucree sandy loam in rangeland. (Color is for air dry soil unless otherwise noted.)

    A--0 to 5 cm; dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) sandy loam, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist; strong very fine and fine granular structure; soft, very friable, nonsticky and nonplastic; many very fine roots; many very fine irregular pores; 10 percent gravel; neutral (pH 7.1); abrupt smooth boundary. 

    BA--5 to 25 cm; dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) sandy loam, very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2) moist; moderat fine and medium subangular blocky structure parting to moderate very fine and fine granular; slightly hard, very friable, nonsticky and nonplastic; common very fine roots; many very fine tubular pores; 10 percent gravel; neutral (pH 7.1); gradual wavy boundary. 

    Bw1--25 to 48 cm; brown (7.5YR 4/2) sandy loam, dark brown (7.5YR 3/3) moist; moderate medium and coarse sbangular blocky structure; hard, very friable, nonsticky and nonplastic; common very fine and few fine roots; common very fine tubular pores; 5 percent gravel; neutral (pH 7.0); gradual wavy boundary. 

    Bw2--48 to 71 cm; brown (7.5YR 4/2) sandy loam, dark brown (7.5YR 3/2) moist; moderate medium and coarse sbangular blocky structure; hard, very friable, nonsticky and nonplastic; common very fine and few fine roots; common very fine tubular pores; 5 percent gravel; neutral (pH 7.0); abrupt smooth boundary. 

    Cr--71 to 74 cm; reddish brown (5YR 5/3), dark reddish brown (5YR 3/3) moist, sandstone that can be brokenin the hands and that breaks down slightly when soaked in water.  Material can be rubbed to a loamy fine sand texture.  (1 to 3 inches thick)

    R--74 cm; reddish brown (5YR 5/3), dark reddish brown (5YR 3/3) moist, sandstone.

TYPE LOCATION: Bear Lake County, Idaho; USGS Giraffe Creek topographic quadrangle; latitude 42.4035833 north, longitude 111.0783333 west, datum WGS84.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING:  
Landform-- ridges, mountain slopes, fan remnants, and hillsides 
Elevation--1,740 to 2,210 meters 
Slope--4 to 30 percent 
Parent material--colluvium or slope alluvium over residuum weathered from sandstone, siltstone, or quartzite
Climate--long, cold winters; cool, moist springs; short, warm summers  
Mean annual precipitation--360 to 580 mm 
Mean annual air temperature--2.8 to 6.0 degrees C
Frost-free period--60 to 85 days

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: None listed.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC OCNDUCTIVITY:  Well drained; 5 to 50 um/sec.

USE AND VEGETATION:  Prucree soils are primarily used for grazing.  The potential native vegetation is mountain big sagebrush and bluebunch wheatgrass.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT:  Prucree soils are of small extent in southeastern Idaho; MLRAs 13 and 43B.

SOIL SURVEY REGIONAL OFFICE (SSRO) RESPONSIBLE: Bozeman, Montana

SERIES ESTABLISHED:  Bear Lake County, Idaho, 2008.  The name is coined from a nearby creek.

REMARKS:  Diagnostic horizons and features recognized in this pedon:
Mollic epipedon--0 to 71 cm (A, BA, Bw1 and Bw2 horizons)

Particle-size control section--25 to 71 cm (Bw1 and Bw2 horizons)

Prucree soils have a frigid temperature regime and a xeric moisture regime.
