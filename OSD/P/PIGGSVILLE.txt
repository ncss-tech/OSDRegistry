TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy-skeletal, artifactic, mixed, superactive, calcareous, mesic Anthroportic Udorthents

TYPICAL PEDON: Piggsville silt loam on a 2 percent slope, in a vacant residential area with fill at an elevation of 207 meters above sea level. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise stated.)

 ^Au1--0 to 15 centimeters (0 to 6 inches); very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2), grayish brown (10YR 5/2), dry; silt loam; weak fine granular structure; friable; common very fine and fine roots throughout; 3 percent gravel; 1 percent cobble-sized brick artifacts, 2 percent channer sized glass artifacts, and 2 percent cobble sized coal combustion byproducts; slightly effervescent; slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); clear wavy boundary (thickness 5 to 25 centimeters)

 ^Bwu1--15 to 56 centimeters (6 to 22 inches); dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2), light brownish gray (10YR 6/2), dry; artifactual silt loam; weak thick platy structure primarily from compaction; friable; few fine roots throughout; 10 percent gravel; 7 percent channer sized glass artifacts, 10 percent gravel sized coal combustion byproducts and 10 percent gravel sized brick artifacts; strongly effervescent; moderately alkaline (pH) 8.0; gradual wavy boundary

 ^Bwu2--56 to 102 centimeters (22 to 40 inches); dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2), light brownish gray (10YR 6/2), dry; very artifactual silt loam; relict weak medium subangular blocky structure; friable; 10 percent gravel; 5 percent channer sized glass artifacts, 10 percent gravel sized coal combustion byproducts, and 15 percent gravel sized brick artifacts; strongly effervescent; moderately alkaline, (pH 8.0); clear smooth boundary; (combined thickness of the ^Bwu horizons is 35 to 100 centimeters)

 ^Cu--102 to 200 centimeters (40 to 79 inches); 60 percent dark brown (10YR 3/3), yellowish brown (10YR 5/4) dry, and 40 percent dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2), light olive brown (2.5Y 5/3) dry; extremely artifactual silt loam; massive; friable;  10 percent cobble sized concrete artifacts, 10 percent cobble sized metal artifacts, 10 percent cobble sized brick artifacts, 10 percent channer size glass artifacts, 15 percent cobble sized artifacts of coal combustion byproducts, and 40 percent cobble sized artifacts of untreated wood; strongly effervescent; moderately alkaline (pH 8.0)

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Milwaukee, Teutonia, and Vieau series which also formed in human-transported material on anthropogenic landforms.

Milwaukee soils formed in fine-silty human-transported material and occur on fills on nearby ground moraines or escarpments
Teutonia soils formed in fine-loamy human-transported material and occur in floodways in nearby drainageways and floodplains.
Vieau soils formed in sandy dredge materials and occur on dredge spoil banks and fills on nearby beach terraces.
