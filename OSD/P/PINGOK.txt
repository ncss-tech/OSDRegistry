GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Bansheecreek, Inigok, Nuiqsut, Poleakoon, and Teshelpuk soils.
Anajuk soils have a histic epipedon and occurs on high centered polygons on patterned ground.
Bansheecreek soils are somewhat poorly drained, lack a glacic layer, lack a mineral contact within 70 cm (28 in), and occur on high relief domes of high-center polygons on relict lakebeds.
Ingok soils are poorly drained, have a histic epipedon, lack a glacic layer, have a cryoturbated horizon, and occur on low relief domes of high centered polygons.
Nuiqsut soils are poorly drained, lack a glacic layer, and occur on the center of low center polygons.
Poleakoon soils have a histic epipedon, lack a glacic layer, and occurs along streams and in string bongs.
Teshekpuk soils are well drained, have a mollic epipedon, lack permafrost, and occur on longitudinal dunes.
