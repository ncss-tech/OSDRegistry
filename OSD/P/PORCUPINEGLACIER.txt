PORCUPINEGLACIER SERIES

Depth class: shallow to lithic bedrock
Drainage class: well drained
Parent material: volcanic ash influenced loamy-skeletal colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock
Landscape: hills
Landform: hillslopes
Slope: 5 to 60 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (74 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C. (37 degrees F.)
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy, mixed, superactive Lithic Haplocryods

TYPICAL PEDON: Porcupineglacier highly organic medial silt loam in a western hemlock feather moss community on a northwest facing convex, linear backslope on a slope of 30 percent at an elevation of 233 m (764 ft).

     Oi--0 to 13 cm (0 to 5 in); slightly decomposed plant material, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; many medium, fine, and coarse roots; extremely acid (pH 4.2) clear wavy boundary.

     A--13 to 16 cm (5 to 6 in); highly organic medial silt loam, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; weak fine granular structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many medium, fine, and coarse roots; very strongly acid (pH 4.5), abrupt wavy boundary.

     E--16 to 18 cm (6 to 7 in); silt loam, brown (7.5YR 4/2) moist, pinkish gray (7.5YR 6/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many medium, fine, and coarse roots; moderately acid (pH 5.9), abrupt wavy boundary.

     Bhs--18 to 24 cm (7 to 9 in); highly organic medial silt loam, very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 4/2) dry; moderate coarse granular structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium roots and many fine roots; moderately acid (pH 5.9), abrupt wavy boundary.

     2C--24 to 40 cm (9 to 16 in); very cobbly silt loam, gray (2.5Y 5/1) moist, light gray (2.5Y 7/1) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and fine roots; 5 percent stones by volume, 10 percent cobbles by volume, 20 percent gravel by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.2), abrupt wavy boundary.

     2R--40 to 65 cm (16 to 26 in); metasedimentary strongly cohernent bedrock.

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.2861111, longitude - -149.3544444, datum WGS84.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING:
Elevation: 0 to 450 meters (0 to 1476 feet)
Parent material: volcanic ash influenced loamy-skeletal colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock
Landform: hillslopes
Slopes: 5 to 60 percent
Mean annual precipitation: 1725 to 2015 mm (68 to 79 in)
Mean annual air temperature: 2 to 4 degrees C. (36 to 39 degrees F.)
Frost free period: 130 to 155 days

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Moose, Hive, and Andacher soils.
Moose soils have a moderately deep paralithic contact and are found on similar landforms on hillslopes.
Hive soils have organic soil horizons throughout, have aquic conditions throughout, lack andic soil properties, lack a spodic horizon, and are found in depressions on hillslopes.
Andacher soils have a deep lithic contact and are found on similar landforms on hillslopes.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:
Drainage class: well
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: very high in the organic surface layer, moderately high to high in the A and Bhs horizons, moderately high in the E and 2C horizons, and very low in the 2R horizon.
Permeability: rapid to very rapid in the organic surface layer, moderate to moderately rapid in the A and Bhs horizons, moderate in the E and 2C horizons, and impermeable in the R horizon.
Runoff: very high

USE AND VEGETATION:
Use: wildlife habitat, recreation, urban land and subsistence.
Native vegetation: western hemlock, western oakfern, splendid feather moss, black crowberry, lingonberry, rusty menziesia, oval-leaf blueberry.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: MLRA 220, Alexander Archipelago-Gulf of Alaska Coast. The series is of small extent.
