Depth class: shallow
Drainage class: very poorly drained
Parent material: organic material over eolian sands
Landscape: coastal plains
Landform: flark string bogs, lakebeds (relict), drainageways, incised streams, and depressions
Slope: 0 to 4 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 130 mm (5 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about -11 degrees C (12 degrees F)
Frost-free period: 22 to 82 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy, mixed, pergelic Typic Historthels

TYPICAL PEDON: Poleakoon mucky peat in a sedge and moss community on a lake shore at an elevation of 43 m (140 ft).

	Oi--0 to 5 cm (0 to 2 in); peat, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) rubbed moist; many very fine roots throughout and few medium roots throughout; neutral, (pH 6.9); clear smooth boundary (4 to 9 cm, 2 to 4 in)

	Oe--5 to 23 cm (2 to 9 in); mucky peat, very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) rubbed moist; many very fine roots throughout and few medium roots throughout; slightly acid, (pH 6.4); clear smooth boundary (15 to 20 cm, 6 to 8 in)

	Oa--23 to 31 cm (9 to 12 in); muck, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) rubbed moist; few very fine roots throughout; slightly acid, (pH 6.4); clear smooth boundary (6 to 10 cm, 2 to 4 in)

	Cgf--31 to 150 cm (12 to 59 in); loamy fine sand, very dark gray (N 3/), rubbed moist; interstitial pores; 20 percent fine dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/6), moist, masses of oxidized iron; neutral, (pH 7.2).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Bansheecreek, Inigok, Kikak, Nuiqsut, and Pingok soils.
Anajuk soils have a cryoturbated horizon and occur on high centered polygons on patterned ground.
Bansheecreek soils are somewhat poorly drained, are organic soils, and occur on high relief domes of high-center polygons on relict lakebeds.
Inigok soils are poorly drained, lack a glacic horizon, and occur on low relief domes on high-center polygons.
Nuiqsut soils are poorly drained, are organic soils, lack a glacic horizon, and occur on the center of low center polygons.
Kikak soils are poorly drained, have permafrost below 100 cm, have an ochric epipedon, and occur on flats of ice wedge polygons.
Pingok soils are organic soils and occur on the high relief troughs of high-center polygons.
