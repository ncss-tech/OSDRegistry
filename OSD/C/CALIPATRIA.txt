CALIPATRIA SERIES

Depth class: Very deep
Drainage class: Poorly drained
Parent material: Lacustrine deposits or alluvium derived from mixed rock sources
Geomorphic location: Playas and basin floors of former lakebeds
Slope range: 0 to 1 percent
Mean annual air temperature: 23 degrees C 
Mean annual precipitation: 77 millimeters 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy over clayey, mixed over smectitic, superactive, hyperthermic Typic Aquisalids

TYPICAL PEDON: Calipatria fine sandy loam on a nearly level and playa at an elevation of -71 meters. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 Cnseg1--0 to 7 centimeters; grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) fine sandy loam; massive; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; nonfluid; 3 percent subangular weakly coherent shell fragments; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderately effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); clear smooth boundary.

 Cnseg2--7 to 22 centimeters; 80 percent olive gray (5Y 5/2) and 20 percent light olive brown (2.5Y 5/3) loam; massive; friable, moderately sticky, moderately plastic; moderately fluid; 2 percent subangular weakly coherent shell fragments; few fine irregular pores; 5 percent medium prominent irregular noncoherent dark reddish brown (5YR 3/4) masses of oxidized iron and 25 percent fine prominent dendritic noncoherent dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/6) pore linings of oxidized iron; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnse--22 to 38 centimeters; olive (5Y 5/3); loamy fine sand; single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; nonfluid; 10 percent subangular weakly coherent shell fragments; 5 percent fine prominent dendritic noncoherent yellowish brown (10YR 5/6) pore linings of oxidized iron; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); abrupt smooth boundary. (5 to 30 centimeters thick)

 C'nseg--38 to 87 centimeters; very dark gray (N 3/); loam; massive; friable, slightly sticky, nonplastic; very fluid; 5 percent subangular weakly coherent shell fragments; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderately effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); clear smooth boundary. (combined thickness of C horizons is 35 to 95 centimeters)

 2Cnseg1--87 to 120 centimeters; brown (7.5YR 4/2); clay; massive; firm, slightly sticky, moderately plastic; nonfluid; 1 percent fine prominent irregular noncoherent yellowish red (5YR 5/8) moist, masses of oxidized iron; moderately effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); clear smooth boundary.

 2Cnseg2--120 to 200 centimeters; brown (7.5YR 4/3) stratified fine sandy loam and loam; massive; friable, slightly sticky, nonplastic; moderately fluid; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.5).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Corvina, Frink, Meloland, Mundo, Saltisun, Slabcity and Wonderstone soils.
Corvina soils have sandy textures throughout the profile and occur on similar adjacent playas.
Frink soils have petrogyspic horizons less than 50 centimeters from the surface, have greater than 70 percent sand throughout the profile, and occur on adjacent playas.
Meloland soils are saturated in the soil moisture control section for less than 30 days, do not have salic horizons, and occur on alluvial fans.
Mundo soils have greater than 18 percent clay in the upper part of the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Saltisun soils 5 to 18 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Slabcity soils have greater than 35 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section, and occur on adjacent playas.
Wonderstone soils have between 18 and 35 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
