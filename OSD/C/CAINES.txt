CAINES SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: volcanic ash influenced coarse-loamy colluvium over gravelly colluvium 
Landscape: mountains 
Landform: mountain slopes 
Slope: 20 to 80 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (74 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C. (37 degrees F.) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Medial over loamy-skeletal, amorphic over mixed, superactive Andic Humicryods

TYPICAL PEDON: Caines medial silt loam in a Sitka spruce community on an east facing linear, linear backslope on a slope of 76 percent at an elevation of 44 m (144 ft).

     Oe--0 to 16 cm (0 to 6 in); moderately decomposed plant material, 60 percent dark reddish brown (2.5YR 3/4) moist, reddish brown (2.5YR 5/4) dry and 40 percent black (2.5Y 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (2.5Y 4/1) dry; many medium and fine roots and common coarse roots; very strongly acid (pH 4.7); clear wavy boundary.

     A--16 to 19 cm (6 to 8 in); medial silt loam, very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 4/2) dry; moderate medium granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and coarse roots and many fine roots; 5 percent gravel by volume; extremely acid (pH 4.4); abrupt wavy boundary.

     E--19 to 23 cm (8 to 9 in); gravelly silt loam, brown (7.5YR 4/2) moist, pinkish gray (7.5YR 6/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine, medium, and coarse roots; 20 percent gravel by volume; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); abrupt wavy boundary.

     Bhs--23 to 37 cm (9 to 15 in); gravelly medial loam, dark reddish brown (5YR 2.5/2) moist, dark reddish gray (5YR 4/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine, medium and coarse roots; 25 percent gravel by volume; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); gradual wavy boundary.

     Bs--37 to 97 cm (15 to 38 in); gravelly medial sandy loam, dark reddish brown (5YR 3/4) moist, reddish brown (5YR 5/4) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and fine roots; 25 percent gravel by volume; moderately acid (pH 5.9); clear wavy boundary.

     C--97 to 200 cm (38 to 79 in); very gravelly sandy loam, olive brown (2.5Y 4/4) moist, light yellowish brown (2.5Y 6/4) dry; massive; friable, slightly sticky, slightly plastic; 3 percent fine faint irregular noncoherent cemented gray (2.5Y 5/1), moist, masses of reduced iron with clear boundaries throughout; 40 percent gravel by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.1).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.0661111, longitude -149.4436111, datum WGS84.

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Aleknagik (AK), Hurdygurdy (AK) and Talkeetna (AK) series. 
Aleknagik soils have an E/A horizon, lack a Bs horizon and have mean annual air temperature less than 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.).
Hurdygurdy soils lack andic soil properties and lack a Bs horizon and have mean annual air temperature less than 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.).
Talkeetna soils lack andic soil properties and have mean annual air temperature less than 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.).

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Elevation: 10 to 600 meters (33 to 1969 feet) 
Parent material: coarse-loamy volcanic ash influenced colluvium over loamy-skeletal colluvium 
Landform: lower third of mountain flanks on mountain slopes 
Slopes: 20 to 80 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: 1725 to 2015 mm (68 to 79 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: 2 to 4 degrees C. (36 to 39 degrees F.) 
Frost free period: 130 to 155 days 

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Castillo soils.  
Castillo soils have up to greater than 60 percent rock fragments throughout the control section, lack a Bhs horizon, and have a lithic contact at depths between 100 to 150 cm (39 to 60 in) and occur on similar landforms on the lower third of mountain flanks. 

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: 
Drainage class: well 
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: very high in the organic surface layer, moderately high in the E horizon, moderately high to high in the A, Bhs, Bs and C horizons 
Permeability: very rapid in the organic surface layer, moderate to moderately rapid in the A, E, and C horizons, moderate to rapid in the Bhs and Bs horizons. 
Runoff: high 

USE AND VEGETATION:  
Use: wildlife habitat, recreation, urban land and subsistence.
Native vegetation: Sitka spruce, Sitka alder, splendid feather moss, common ladyfern, bunchberry dogwood, western oakfern, rusty menziesia, devilsclub, claspleaf twistedstalk, squashberry, Girgensohn's sphagnum. 

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: MLRA 220, Alexander Archipelago-Gulf of Alaska Coast. The series is of small extent.
