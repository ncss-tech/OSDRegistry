CORVINA SERIES

Depth class: Very deep
Drainage class: Poorly drained

Geomorphic location: Playas and basin floors
Slope range: 0 to 1 percent
Mean annual air temperature: 23 degrees C

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy, mixed, hyperthermic Typic Aquisalids

TYPICAL PEDON: Corvina stratified shelly fine sand and fine sandy loam, on a nearly level playa at an elevation of -73 meters. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 Cn--0 to 6 centimeters; 80 percent dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/4) and 20 percent very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1); stratified shelly fine sand, shelly fine sandy loam; massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 25 percent weakly coherent shell fragments; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.7); clear smooth boundary. (0 to 30 centimeters thick)

 Cnseg1--6 to 25 centimeters; black (N 2.5/) moist; fine sand; massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 12 percent medium prominent dark yellowish brown (10YR 3/4) masses of oxidized iron; 5 percent weakly coherent shell fragments; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnseg2--25 to 38 centimeters; black (5Y 2.5/1); loamy sand; massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 5 percent weakly coherent shell fragments; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.7); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnseg3--38 to 60 centimeters; 90 percent black (N 2.5/), and 10 percent very dark gray (5Y 3/1); fine sand; single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 7.9); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnseg4--60 to 85 centimeters; brown (7.5YR 4/3); fine sandy loam; massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 2 percent weakly coherent shell fragments; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); clear smooth boundary.

 Cnseg5--85 to 140 centimeters; dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2); loamy fine sand; massive; friable, slightly sticky, nonplastic; few fine tubular pores; 2 percent medium distinct noncoherent brown (7.5YR 5/2) iron depletions and 5 percent medium prominent irregular strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) masses of oxidized iron; 2 percent weakly coherent shell fragments; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); clear smooth boundary.

 Cnseg6--140 to 200 centimeters; dark gray (10YR 4/1); loamy sand; single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.5).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: 
These are the Calipatria, Frink, Mundo, Saltisun, Slabcity and Wonderstone soils.
Calipatria soils have coarse-loamy over clayey textures in the particle-control section and occur on adjacent landforms.
Frink soils have a petrogyspic horizon and are on adjacent landforms.
Mundo soils have fine-loamy over clayey textures in the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent landforms.
Saltisun soils have coarse-loamy textures throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent landforms.
Slabcity soils have clay textures and occur on adjacent landforms.
Wonderstone soils have greater than 18 percent clay throughout the profile and occur on adjacent landforms.
