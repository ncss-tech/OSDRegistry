 Bw--30 to 58 centimeters; ashy paragravelly loamy sand; light brown (7.5YR 6/3), brown (7.5YR 4/2), moist; 3 percent field estimated clay; weak fine subangular blocky structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; few very fine roots throughout; many fine low-continuity interstitial pores; 8 percent subrounded andesite gravel, 15 percent subrounded pumice paragravel; slightly acid (pH 6.1); clear wavy boundary

 C--58 to 157 centimeters; paragravelly ashy sand; light brown (7.5YR 6/4), brown (7.5YR 4/4), moist; single grain; loose; nonsticky, nonplastic; few fine roots throughout; many fine low-continuity interstitial pores; 8 percent subrounded andesite gravel, 15 percent subrounded pumice paragravel; neutral (pH 6.6)
