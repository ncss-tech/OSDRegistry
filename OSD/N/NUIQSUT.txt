GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Bansheecreek, Inigok, Kikak, Pingok, and Poleakoon soils.
Anajuk soils are very poorly drained have a histic epipedon, and occurs on high centered polygons on patterned ground.
Bansheecreek soils are somewhat poorly drained, do no have a mineral contact within 70 cm (28 in), and occur on high relief domes of high-center polygons on relict lakebeds.
Ingok soils have a histic epipedon, have a glacic layer, have a cryoturbated horizon, and occur on low relief domes of high centered polygons.
Kikak soils have permafrost below 100 cm, have an ochric epipedon, and occur on flats of ice wedge polygons.
Pingok soils are very poorly drained, have a glacic horizon, are dominated by hemic materials, and occur on high relief troughs of high-centered polygons.
Poleakoon soils are very poorly drained, have a histic epipedon, and occurs along streams and in string bongs.
