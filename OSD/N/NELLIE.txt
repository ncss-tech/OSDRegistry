NELLIE SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: somewhat poorly drained 
Parent material: coarse-loamy alluvium over sandy and gravelly alluvium derived from metasedimentary rock 
Landscape: valleys 
Landform: flood plains 
Slope: 0 to 2 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (72.9 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37.4 F) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy over sandy or sandy-skeletal, mixed, superactive, nonacid Typic Cryaquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Nellie highly very fine sandy loam in a maritime shrub low floodplain community on a linear, linear floodplain on a slope of 1 percent at an elevation of 15 m (49 ft) 

     Oi--0 to 1 cm (0 to 0.5 in); slightly decomposed plant material, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist, dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) dry; very strongly acid (pH 4.5), abrupt smooth boundary 

     A--1 to 7 cm (0.5 to 3 in); very fine sandy loam, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist, dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) dry; moderate fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many fine and medium roots and common coarse roots; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); clear smooth boundary 

     C--7 to 32 cm (3 to 13 in); very fine sandy loam, black (5Y 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (5Y 4/1) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine, medium, and coarse roots; 2 percent medium prominent irregular noncoherent cemented dark brown (7.5YR 3/4) masses of oxidized iron with clear boundaries infused into matrix adjacent to pores; strongly acid (pH 5.3); diffuse smooth boundary 

     Cg1--32 to 54 cm (13 to 21 in); very fine sandy loam, dark greenish gray (10Y 3/1) moist, greenish gray (10Y 5/1) dry; structureless massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine and medium roots; 2 percent fine prominent threadlike very weakly coherent cemented strong brown (7.5YR 4/6), moist, masses of oxidized iron with sharp boundaries lining pores and 10 percent medium prominent irregular noncoherent cemented dark brown (7.5YR 3/4), moist, masses of oxidized iron with clear boundaries infused into matrix adjacent to pores; slightly acid (pH 6.2); diffuse smooth boundary 

     Cg2--54 to 72 cm (21 to 28 in); very fine sandy loam, dark greenish gray (10Y 3/1) moist, greenish gray (10Y 5/1) dry; structureless massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine roots; 1 percent medium prominent irregular noncoherent cemented dark brown (7.5YR 3/4), moist, masses of oxidized iron with sharp boundaries infused into matrix adjacent to pores; slightly acid (pH 6.2); gradual smooth boundary 

     2Cg3--72 to 100 cm (28 to 39 in); extremely gravelly coarse sand, dark greenish gray (10Y 3/1) moist, variegated greenish gray (10Y 5/1) dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; 20 percent rounded gravels by volume, 60 percent rounded gravels by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.2) 

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska: latitude 60.1277778, longitude -149.3886111, datum WGS84

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Bulldog (AK), Niklavar (AK), and Swedna (AK) series. 
Bulldog soils occur on beach plains and have pHs ranging to slightly alkaline 
Niklavar soils have a mean annual precipitation ranging from 381 to 762 mm (15 to 30 in) 
Swedna soils have a mean annual air temperature of -3 degrees C (26 degrees F) and mean annual precipitation of 406 mm (16 in) 

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Elevation: 2 to 95 meters (7 to 312 feet)

Landform: flood plains 
Slopes: 0 to 2 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: 1725 to 2015 mm (68 to 79 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: 2 to 4 degrees C. (36 to 39 degrees F.) 
Frost free period: 130 to 155 days 

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Humpy and Squab soils. 
Humpy soils lack a gravelly substratum and occur on similar landforms on floodplains. 
Squab soils have a sandy skeletal particle size class and occur on similar landforms on floodplains. 

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:

Permeability: rapid for Oi horizon, moderate to rapid for A, C, and Cg horizons, rapid for 2Cg3 horizon. 
Runoff: very high 
Flooding: rare to frequent, brief 
Ponding: none
