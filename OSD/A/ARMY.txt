ARMY SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: sandy and gravelly alluvium derived from metasedimentary rock 
Landscape: bajadas, fan piedmonts 
Landform: alluvial fans, flood plains 
Slope: 1 to 8 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (73 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37 F) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy-skeletal, mixed Typic Cryofluvents

TYPICAL PEDON: Army highly organic medial silt loam in a maritime shrub community on a south, southwest facing linear, convex fan on a slope of 3 percent at an elevation of 60m (197 ft).

     Oe--0 to 3 cm (0 to 1 in); moderately decomposed plant material, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; common medium roots and common fine roots; extremely acid (pH 3.8); abrupt smooth boundary.

     A--3 to 13 cm (1 to 5 in); very fine sandy loam, very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2) moist, grayish brown (10YR 5/2) dry; moderate fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many medium and fine roots and common coarse roots; very strongly acid (pH 4.5); clear smooth boundary.

     C1--13 to 30 cm (5 to 12 in); very gravelly loamy sand, dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) moist, light brownish gray (2.5Y 6/2) dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium roots and many fine roots; 5 percent angular channers by volume, 15 percent subrounded cobbles by volume, 25 percent subrounded gravel by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.1); gradual smooth boundary.

     C2--30 to 200 cm (12 to 79 in); extremely gravelly sand, dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) moist, variegated light brownish gray (2.5Y 6/2) dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine roots; 10 percent angular channers by volume, 15 percent subrounded cobbles by volume, 50 percent subrounded gravel by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.2).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska: latitude 60.1827778, longitude -149.3786111, datum WGS84

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Chenega (AK), Hollow (AK), Ironcreek (AK), Kidazqeni (AK), Malmesbury (AK), Shovelcreek (AK), Stratify (AK), Togiak (AK), and Willowriver (AK) series.
Chenega soils are somewhat excessively drained.
Hollow soils have precipitation ranging from 533 to 1651 mm (21 to 65 in).
Ironcreek soils have precipitation ranging 300 to 800 mm (12 to 31 in).
Kidazqeni soils are moderately well to well drained and have precipitation ranging from 406 to 889 mm (16 to 35 in).
Malmesbury soils have buried O horizons.
Shovelcreek soils have mean annual air temperatures ranging from -6 to -1 degrees C (21 to 30 degrees F) and precipitation ranging from 300 to 500 mm (12 to 20 in).
Stratify soils have a mean annual soil temperature less than 2 degrees C (36 degrees F).
Togiak soils have precipitation ranging from 700 to 800 mm (28 to 31 in) and mean annual air temperature ranging from 0.9 to 1.7 degrees C (34 to 35 degrees F).
Willowriver soils have buried horizons and precipitation ranging from 300 to 500 mm (12 to 20 in).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Spruce series.
Spruce soils have a coarse loamy over sandy or sandy-skeletal particle size class and occur on similar landforms on talfs.
 
DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:

Runoff: low 
Flooding: rare to frequent, very brief
