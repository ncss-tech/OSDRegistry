GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Arrakis, Bodfish, Kikak, and Tuluga soils.
Anajuk soils are very poorly drained have a histic epipedon, and occur on high centered polygons on patterned ground.
Arrakis soils are excessively drained, lack permafrost, have an ochric epipedon, and occur on top of dunes and meander scrolls.
Bodfish soils lack permafrost, have an ochric epipedon, and occur on flood plains.
Kikak soils are poorly drained, have permafrost below 100 cm, have an ochric epipedon, and occur on flats of ice wedge polygons.
Tuluga soils are poorly drained, lack permafrost, have aquic conditions, and occur on depressions on stream terraces.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: 
Drainage class: somewhat poorly drained

Permeability: Moderately rapid in the Oi and A horizons, very rapid in the Cg and Cg/Oe horizons, and very slow in the Cgf horizon.
