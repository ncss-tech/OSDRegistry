AIALIK SERIES

Depth class: shallow to bedrock, lithic 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: loamy-skeletal colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock 
Landscape: mountains 
Landform: mountain slopes 
Slope: 20 to 90 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 2640 mm (103 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 1 degrees C (34 F) 
Frost-free period: 25 to 50 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy-skeletal, mixed, superactive Lithic Humicryepts

TYPICAL PEDON: Aialik very channery highly organic silt loam in an alpine dwarf scrub community on an east facing concave, linear backslope on a slope of 30 percent at an elevation of 755m (2477 ft).

     Oe--0 to 2 cm (0 to 1 in); moderately decomposed plant material, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; many medium, fine, and coarse roots; extremely acid (pH 4.3); abrupt broken boundary.

     A--2 to 7 cm (1 to 3 in); very channery highly organic silt loam, dark brown (7.5YR 3/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 5/2) dry; moderate fine granular structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and coarse roots and many fine roots; 38 percent angular metasedimentary, channers, flagstones, and stones by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.8); clear wavy boundary.

     Bw--7 to 35 cm (3 to 14 in); very channery highly organic loam, dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) moist, light brownish gray (10YR 6/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and fine roots; 45 percent angular metasedimentary, channers, flagstones, and stones by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.3); abrupt wavy boundary.

     R--35 to 60 cm (14 to 24 in); strongly coherent metasedimentary bedrock. 

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska: latitude 60.1183333, longitude -149.3263889, datum WGS84

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Ferebee (AK), Shermount (WA), and Teewinot (WY) series. 
Ferebee soils have a higher mean annual precipitation ranging from 3048 to 7620 mm (120 to 300in) and have alpine tundra vegetation. 
Shermount soils have a lower mean annual precipitation of 635 to 889 mm (25 to 35 in). 
Teewinot soils have a lower mean annual precipitation of 762 to 1524 mm (30 to 60 in) and sandy loam texture class.
