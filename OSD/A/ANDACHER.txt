ANDACHER SERIES

Depth class: deep to bedrock, paralithic 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: volcanic ash loamy-skeletal colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock 
Landscape: hills 
Landform: hillslopes 
Slope: 12 to 65 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (73 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37 degrees F) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Medial-skeletal, amorphic Andic Humicryods

TYPICAL PEDON: Andacher flaggy highly organic medial silt loam in a maritime forest community on an east facing linear, linear backslope on a slope of 27 percent at an elevation of 180m (591 ft).

     Oi--0 to 2 cm (0 to 1 in); slightly decomposed plant material, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; common medium roots and many fine roots; extremely acid (pH 4.2); abrupt wavy boundary.

     Oa--2 to 4 cm (1 to 2 in); highly decomposed plant material, black (7.5YR 2/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; moderate fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and coarse roots and many fine roots; extremely acid (pH 4.2); abrupt wavy boundary.

     E--4 to 9 cm (2 to 4 in); silt, dark brown (7.5YR 3/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 5/2) dry; moderate thin platy structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and fine roots; 2 percent by volume angular metasedimentary gravels; strongly acid (pH 5.2); abrupt broken boundary.

     Bhs--9 to 14 cm (4 to 6 in); highly organic medial silt loam, 60 percent very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 4/2) dry and 40 percent black (10YR 2/1) moist, dark gray (10YR 4/1) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many medium roots and common fine and coarse roots; 5 percent by volume angular metasedimentary gravels; strongly acid (pH 5.3); abrupt wavy boundary.

     E'--14 to 18 cm (6 to 7 in); very flaggy silt loam, 60 percent gray (7.5YR 5/1) moist, gray (7.5YR 6/1) dry and 40 percent, black (2.5Y 2.5/1) moist, dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and fine roots; 50 percent by volume angular metasedimentary channers and flagstones; very strongly acid (pH 4.6); abrupt wavy boundary.

     B'hs--18 to 62 cm (7 to 24 in); extremely channery highly organic medial silt loam, black (5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (5YR 4/1) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine, medium, and coarse roots; 63 percent angular metasedimentary channers by volume; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); abrupt wavy boundary.

     Bs--62 to 85 cm (24 to 34 in); very cobbly medial sandy loam, 80 percent dark brown (7.5YR 3/3) moist, brown (7.5YR 5/3) dry and 20 percent olive brown (2.5Y 4/3) moist, light yellowish brown (2.5Y 6/3) dry; weak fine subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine, medium, and coarse roots; 55 percent angular metasedimentary gravels and cobbles by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.4); clear wavy boundary.

     BC--85 to 105 cm (34 to 41 in); very cobbly medial sandy loam, yellowish brown (10YR 5/4) moist, light yellowish brown (10YR 6/4) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 55 percent angular metasedimentary gravels and cobbles by volume; moderately acid (pH 5.8); clear wavy boundary.

     Cr--105 to 140 cm (41 to 55 in); moderately coherent paralithic bedrock 

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska: latitude 60.2597222, longitude -149.3458333, datum WGS84

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Humskel (WA) , Saar (WA), and Thader (OR) series. 
Humskel soils have lithic contact at 58 cm (23 in).
Saar soils have densic contact of glacial till at depths of 66 to 24 cm (26 to 60 in).
Thader soils have lithic contact at 84cm (33 in).

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Elevation: 3 to 405 meters (10 to 1329 feet) 
Parent material: loamy-skeletal volcanic ash colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock 
Landform: hillslopes 
Slopes: 20 to 90 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: 1725 to 2015 mm (68 to 79 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: -1 to 2 degrees C. (30 to 36 degrees F.) 
Frost free period: 130 to 155 days 

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Hive, Moose, and Porcupineglacier soils. 
Hive soils have organic soil material to 200cm (78 in) and occur on similar landforms on backslope and toeslope positions. 
Moose soils are moderately deep and occur on similar landforms on backslope, shoulder, and toeslope positions.
Porcupineglacier soils are shallow and occur on similar landforms on summits, shoulders, and backslope positions. 

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: 
Drainage class: well 
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: moderately high to very high for O horizons, moderately high to high for E, B, and BC horizons, very low to moderately high for Cr horizons.
Permeability: moderate to rapid for O, E, B, and BC horizons, very slow to moderate for Cr horizons.
Runoff: medium 

USE AND VEGETATION: 
Use: wildlife habitat, recreation, and subsistence.
Native vegetation: Sitka alder, splendid feather moss, Sitka spruce, Schreber's big red stem moss.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: MLRA 220, Alexander Archipelago-Gulf of Alaska Coast. The series is of small extent.
