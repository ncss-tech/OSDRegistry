ASCENSION SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: very poorly drained 
Parent material: organic material over loamy residuum weathered from metamorphic rock and/or loamy slope alluvium 
Landscape: mountains 
Landform: depressions 
Slope: 2 to 8 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (73 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37 degrees F) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy, mixed, euic Terric Cryohemists

TYPICAL PEDON: Ascension peat in a maritime scrubland peat plain depression on a south facing concave, linear depression on a slope of 2 percent at an elevation of 255m (837 ft).

     Oi1--0 to 11 cm (0 to 4 in); peat, light olive brown (2.5Y 5/6) moist, olive yellow (2.5Y 6/6) dry; many fine roots; strongly acid (pH 5.2); gradual smooth boundary.

     Oi2--11 to 19 cm (4 to 8 in); peat, dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/4) moist, light yellowish brown (10YR 6/4) dry; common medium roots and many fine roots; very strongly acid (pH 4.5); gradual smooth boundary.

     Oe1--19 to 34 cm (8 to 13 in); mucky peat, dark brown (10YR 3/3) moist, brown (10YR 5/3) dry; common fine roots; very strongly acid (pH 4.7); gradual smooth boundary.

     Oe2--34 to 67 cm (13 to 26 in); mucky peat, dark brown (7.5YR 3/3) moist, brown (7.5YR 5/3) dry; common fine roots; very strongly acid (pH 4.8); gradual smooth boundary.

     Oe3--67 to 90 cm (26 to 35 in); mucky peat, very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 4/2) dry; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); gradual smooth boundary.

     A--90 to 200 cm (35 to 79 in); channery mucky silt, black (10YR 2/1) moist, dark gray (10YR 4/1) dry; structureless massive; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 20 percent by volume angular mixed channers; strongly acid (pH 5.1).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska, : latitude 60.1044444, longitude -149.3466667, datum WGS84

Some pedons have mineral contact outside of the limits for a terric subgroup. Some pedons have thicker fibric or sapric material than hemic. Some pedons have a C, Cg, or Ag horizon below the histic epipedon. 

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Aginaktuk (AK), Doroshin (AK), Grice (ID), Hewitt (AK), Mineralcreek (AK), Mosquitopoint (AK), Orgter (AK), Pellernarquq (AK), Runkelscreek, Stanbro (OR), Tacaes. Tepete, Luchek (AK), Nuka (AK).
Aginaktuk soils have up to 27 percent clay in the mineral substratum, a mean annual air temperature of -5 to -1 degrees C (23 to 30 degrees F).
Doroshin soils have textures ranging to sandy loam in the mineral substratum and annual precipitation ranging from 6 to 11 cm (14 to 29 in).
Grice soils have a thinner organic cap with a maximum thickness of 53 cm (21 in) and occur on elevations from 671 to 1463 cm (2,200 to 4,800 ft).
Hewitt soils have up to 35 percent clay in the mineral substratum and have a mean annual air temperature of -1 to 4 degrees C (30 to 40 degrees F).
Mineralcreek soils are somewhat poorly drained.
Mosquitopoint soils have isotic minerology and a peraquic soil moisture regime.
Orgter soils soils have isotic minerology and occur on stream terraces.
Pellernarquq soils are formed in loess.
Luchek soils have a frost free period of 20 to 50 cm.
Runkelscreek soils have ponding, have mineral substratum textures of loam and very fine sandy loam.
Stanbro soils have up to 35 percent clay in the mineral substratum.
Tacaes soils have dark grey (N 4/) matrix in mineral substratum and occur on glaciolacustrine deposits.
Tepete soils have up to 35 percent clay in the mineral substratum and have a mean annual air temperature of 1 to 6 degrees C (33.8 to 42.8 degrees F).
Luchek soils have a frost free period of 20 to 50 cm.
Nuka soils have glacial till below the organic epipedon.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Elevation: 40 to 580 meters (131 to 1903 feet) 
Parent material: organic material over loamy residuum weathered from diorite and/or loamy slope alluvium 
Landform: depressions 
Slopes: 2 to 8 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: 1725 to 2015 mm (68 to 79 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: 2 to 4 degrees C. (36 to 39 degrees F.) 
Frost free period: 130 to 155 days 

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Callisto and Blying soils.
Blying soils are well drained and have lithic contact at a depth of 17 to 50 cm (7 to 20 in).
Callisto soils are well drained, have andic soil properties, and lithic contact at a depth of 70 to 200 cm (28 to 79 in).

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: 
Drainage class: very poorly drained 
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: moderately high to high in all horizons 
Permeability: moderate to rapid permeability in all horizons 
Runoff: negligible 

USE AND VEGETATION: 
Use: wildlife habitat, recreation, and subsistence.
Native vegetation: sedge, black crowberry, bog laurel, lodgepole pine, sphagnum.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: MLRA 220, Alexander Archipelago-Gulf of Alaska Coast. The series is of small extent.
