SANDSPIT SERIES

Depth class: very deep
Drainage class: poorly drained
Parent material: sandy and gravelly alluvium
Landscape: valleys
Landform: flood plains
Slope: 0 to 3 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (72.9 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37.4 F)
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy-skeletal, mixed Typic Cryaquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Sandspit silt in a maritime shrub community on a linear, linear low floodplain on a slope of 1 percent at an elevation of 2 m (7 ft).

     Oa--0 to 11 cm (0 to 4 in); highly decomposed plant material, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist, dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) dry; many very fine and fine roots and common medium roots; neutral (pH 7.1); abrupt smooth boundary.

     Cg1--11 to 20 cm (4 to 8 in); silt, dark gray (N 4/), moist, gray (N 6/), dry; structureless massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many very fine and fine roots and common medium roots; neutral (pH 7.1); abrupt smooth boundary.

     Cg2--20 to 200 cm (8 to 79 in); extremely gravelly coarse sand, very dark gray (N 3/), moist, gray (N 5/), dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; 15 percent cobbles rounded by volume, 60 percent gravels rounded by volume; neutral (pH 7.2).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska: latitude 60.1472222, longitude -149.4513889, datum WGS84

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Anan (AK), Astrolable (AK), Eachuston (CO, MT), Jurvannah (ID), Longarm (AK), Squab (AK), Uliktuk (AK), and Ustiugof (AK) series.
Anan soils have a mean annual precipitation of 2794 mm (110 in) and mean annual air temperature of 41 degrees.
Astrolable soils occur on tidal marshes and low floodplains of shore complexes.
Eachuston soils have a mean annual precipitation of 250mm (9.8 in) and mean annual air temperature of 12 degrees C (54 degrees F).
Jurvannah soils have a mean annual precipitation of 559 to 610 mm (22 to 24 in) and frost-free days ranging from 50 to 70 days.
Longarm soils have a slightly acid pH in mineral horizons.
Squab soils lack a reduced matrix.
Uliktuk soils have a frost-free period of 70 to 90 days and mean annual air temperature of -4 degrees C (25 degrees F).
Ustiugof soils occur on coastal plains.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Spruce and Iron soils.
Spruce soils have a coarse-loamy over sandy or sandy-skeletal particle size class and occur on similar landforms on floodplains and alluvial fans.
Iron soils are well drained and occur on similar landforms on floodplains.
