TAXONOMIC CLASS: Fine, smectitic, hyperthermic Typic Aquisalids

TYPICAL PEDON: Slabcity shelly sandy loam on a nearly level playa. When described the soil was moist throughout. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 Cnseg1--0 to 16 centimeters; grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2), light gray (2.5Y 7/2), dry; shelly sandy loam; massive; slightly hard, very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic, nonfluid; many very fine irregular pores; 20 percent shell fragments; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); strongly alkaline (pH 8.8); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnseg2--16 to 19 centimeters; dark greenish gray (10Y 4/1) clay; weak medium platy structure; extremely hard, very firm, moderately sticky, very plastic, slightly fluid; common very fine irregular pores; 60 percent distinct pressure faces in cracks; 8 percent medium distinct noncoherent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) masses of oxidized iron and 8 percent medium distinct noncoherent gray (7.5YR 5/1) and dark brown (7.5YR 3/2) iron depletions; positive color change (3 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 8.3); abrupt smooth boundary. (Combined thickness of the Cnseg horizons is 10 to 150 cm thick)

 Cny--19 to 200 centimeters; brown (7.5YR 4/4); clay; massive; extremely hard, very firm, moderately sticky, very plastic, nonfluid; few very fine irregular pores; 60 percent distinct pressure faces in cracks; 8 percent medium distinct noncoherent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) masses of oxidized iron and 10 percent medium prominent noncoherent gray (10YR 5/1) iron depletions; 1 percent medium distinct platy strongly coherent cemented gypsum crystals; positive color change (3 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 8.4).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Calipatria, Imperial, Mundo, Niland and Wonderstone soils.
Calipatria soils have less than 18 percent clay in the upper part of the particle-size control section, are formed in mixed over smectitic parent material, and occur on adjacent playas.
Imperial soils do not have salic horizons, are saturated in the soil moisture control section for less than 30 days, and occur on higher terraces of lakebeds.
Mundo soils have 18 to 35 percent clay in the upper part of the particle-size control section, and occur on adjacent playas.
Niland soils do not have salic horizons, have sandy textures in the upper part of the particle-size control section, and occur on nearby floodplains.
Saltisun soils: have less than 18 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Wonderstone soils have 18 to 35 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
