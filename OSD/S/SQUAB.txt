SQUAB SERIES

Depth class: very deep
Drainage class: poorly drained
Parent material: sandy and gravelly alluvium
Landscape: valleys
Landform: flood plains
Slope: 0 to 4 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (72.9 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37.4 F)
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy-skeletal, mixed Typic Cryaquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Squab very fine sandy loam in a maritime shrub community on a linear, linear low floodplain on a slope of 1 percent at an elevation of 15m (49 ft).

     A--0 to 10 cm (0 to 4 in); very fine sandy loam, very dark gray (10YR 3/1) moist, gray (10YR 5/1) dry; weak medium granular structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium and coarse roots and many fine roots; 7 percent medium prominent irregular noncoherent cemented dark brown (7.5YR 3/2), moist, masses of oxidized iron with diffuse boundaries throughout; 10 percent rounded gravels by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.2); abrupt wavy boundary.

     Cg1--10 to 22 cm (4 to 9 in); extremely gravelly sand, very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1) moist, variegated gray (2.5Y 5/1) dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine roots; 2 percent rounded cobbles by volume, 68 percent rounded gravels by volume; moderately acid (pH 6.0); clear wavy boundary.

     Cg2--22 to 47 cm (9 to 19 in); stratified gravelly sand to fine sandy loam, very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1) moist, variegated gray (2.5Y 5/1) dry; structureless massive; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine and medium roots; and 10 percent coarse prominent irregular noncoherent cemented (7.5yr 3/3), moist, masses of oxidized iron with clear boundaries throughout; 1 percent rounded cobbles by volume, 15 percent rounded gravels by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.3); clear wavy boundary.

     Cg3--47 to 200 cm (19 to 79 in); extremely gravelly sand, very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1) moist, variegated gray (2.5Y 5/1) dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; 5 percent rounded cobbles by volume, 75 percent rounded gravels by volume; slightly acid (pH 6.4).

COMPETING SERIES: COMPETING SERIES: These are the Anan (AK), Astrolable (AK), Eachuston (CO, MT), Jurvannah (ID), Longarm (AK), Sandspit (AK), Uliktuk (AK), and Ustiugof (AK) series.
Anan soils have a mean annual precipitation of 2794 mm (110 in) and mean annual air temperature of 41 degrees.
Astrolable soils have pH ranges to slightly alkaline and occur on tidal marshes and low floodplains of shore complexes.
Eachuston soils have a mean annual precipitation of 250mm (9.8 in) and mean annual air temperature of 12 degrees C (54 degrees F).
Jurvannah soils have a mean annual precipitation of 559 to 610 mm (22 to 24 in) and frost-free days ranging from 50 to 70 days.
Longarm soils have a slightly acid pH in mineral horizons.
Sandspit soils have a silty cap with fewer than 30 percent coarse fragments.
Uliktuk soils have a Bw horizon.
Ustiugof soils have coarse-loamy cap with fewer than 10 percent coarse fragments, have pHs ranging to slightly alkaline, and occur on coastal plains.
