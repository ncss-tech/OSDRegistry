SKI SERIES

Depth class: shallow to lithic bedrock
Drainage class: poorly drained
Parent material: volcanic ash coarse-loamy colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock
Landscape: mountains
Landform: mountain slopes
Slope: 20 to 40 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 2380 mm (94 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.)
Frost-free period: 65 to 100 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Medial, amorphic over mixed, superactive, acid Lithic Cryaquands

TYPICAL PEDON: Ski channery mucky medial silt loam in a subalpine shrubby herbaceous meadow community on a west facing linear, convex center third mountainflank on a slope of 20 percent at an elevation of 480m (1575 ft).

     Oe--0 to 4 cm (0 to 2 in); moderately decomposed plant material, very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 4/2) dry; many medium and fine roots and common coarse roots; extremely acid (pH 4.4); clear smooth boundary.

     Oa--4 to 10 cm (2 to 4 in); medial highly decomposed plant material, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist, dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) dry; many medium and fine roots and common coarse roots; 10 percent angular metasedimentary channers by volume; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); very strongly alkaline (NaF pH 9.1); 74 percent volcanic glass; clear wavy boundary.

     Bg1--10 to 32 cm (4 to 13 in); channery mucky medial silt loam, very dark grayish brown (2.5Y 3/2) moist, grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, slightly sticky, slightly plastic; common medium and fine roots; 5 percent medium prominent platy dark reddish brown (5YR 3/4), moist, masses of oxidized iron with sharp boundaries on faces of peds and 15 percent medium faint irregular dark gray (2.5Y 4/1), moist, masses of reduced iron with diffuse boundaries throughout; 10 percent angular metasedimentary channers by volume, 24 percent angular metasedimentary flagstones by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.9); very strongly alkaline (NaF pH 11.2); 20 percent volcanic glass; gradual wavy boundary.

     Bg2--32 to 50 cm (13 to 20 in); channery medial silt loam, 60 percent dark olive brown (2.5Y 3/3) moist, light olive brown (2.5Y 5/3) dry and 25 percent dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) moist, light brownish gray (2.5Y 6/2) dry and 15 percent very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2) moist grayish brown (10YR 5/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, slightly sticky, slightly plastic; common medium and fine roots; 2 percent medium distinct irregular light yellowish brown (2.5Y 6/4), moist, masses of oxidized iron with clear boundaries throughout and 15 percent fine prominent platy dark reddish brown (5YR 3/4), moist, masses of oxidized iron with sharp boundaries on faces of peds; 5 percent angular metasedimentary channers by volume, 13 percent angular metasedimentary flagstones by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.3); very strongly alkaline (NaF pH 11.7); abrupt wavy boundary.

     R--50 to 75 cm (20 to 30 in); strongly coherent metasedimentary bedrock.

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.1172222, longitude -149.3338889, datum WGS84.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING:
Elevation: 200 to 900 meters (656 to 2953 feet)
Parent material: volcanic ash coarse-loamy colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock
Landform: mountain slopes

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Exit soils.
Exit soils have an albic horizon, have a spodic horizon, and lack a reduced matrix and occur on lower third and upper third mountainflanks.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:
Drainage class: poorly drained
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: very high in the Oe horizon, moderately high to very high in the Oa, Bg1 and Bg2 horizons, and very low to moderately low in the R horizon
Permeability: rapid to very rapid in the Oe horizon, moderate to very rapid in the Oa horizon, moderate to rapid in the Bg1 and Bg2 horizons, and impermeable to very slow in the R horizon
Runoff: very high

Use: wildlife habitat, recreation, and subsistence.
