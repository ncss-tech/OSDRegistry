Depth class: Very deep

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy, mixed, superactive, hyperthermic Typic Aquisalids

TYPICAL PEDON: Saltisun fine sandy loam on a nearly level and recently exposed playa at an elevation of -72 meters. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 Cnseg1--0 to 30 centimeters; 90 percent dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2)  and 10 percent grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2); fine sandy loam; massive; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic, nonfluid; 8 percent coarse distinct gray (5Y 6/1) iron depletions and 15 percent coarse prominent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) masses of oxidized iron; 5 percent nonflat subrounded weakly coherent shell fragments; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 7.9); abrupt smooth boundary

 Cnseg2--30 to 52 centimeters; 60 percent dark greenish gray (10Y 4/1) and 40 percent olive (5Y 4/3); stratified fine sandy loam and silt loam; massive; very friable, slightly sticky, slightly plastic, slightly fluid; 10 percent fine prominent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) masses of oxidized iron; slight sulfurous odor; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 8.0); abrupt smooth boundary

 Cnseg3--52 to 71 centimeters; dark greenish gray (10Y 4/1); loam; massive; friable, slightly sticky, nonplastic, slightly fluid; 20 percent coarse prominent dendritic olive (5Y 5/3) masses of oxidized iron; slight sulfurous odor; color change (3 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); abrupt smooth boundary

 Cnseg4--71 to 122 centimeters; dark greenish gray (10Y 4/1); silt loam; massive; friable, moderately sticky, slightly plastic, slightly fluid; 20 percent coarse prominent olive (5Y 5/3) masses of oxidized iron; slight sulfurous odor; color change (3 percent hydrogen peroxide); slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); abrupt smooth boundary

 Cnseg5--122 to 200 centimeters; dark greenish gray (5GY 4/1); silt loam; massive; friable, moderately sticky, slightly plastic, slightly fluid; moderate sulfurous odor; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 7.9)

COMPETING SERIES:

Bunkerhill soils have laminar layers of silica cemented sand grains within a depth of 50 centimeters of the soil surface.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: 
These are the Calipatria, Indio, Mundo, Naas, and Wonderstone soils

Calipatria soils have clay textures within 100 cm of the soil surface and occur on adjacent playas.
Indio soils do not have a salic horizons and occur on alluvial fans and floodplains.
Mundo soils have a contrasting particle-size control section with clay textures lower in the profile, are formed in mixed over smectitic parent material, and occur on adjacent playas.
Naas soils have a sandy particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas, beach ridges, and areas with overblown sand.
Wonderstone soils have greater than 18 percent clay within the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
