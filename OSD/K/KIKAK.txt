GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Arrakis, Atigaru, Bodfish, Polakoon, and Tuluga soils.
Anajuk soils are very poorly drained, have permafrost, have cryoturbated and glacic horizons, and occur on low relief troughs of high-center polygons.
Arrakis soils have a regular decrease in organic material by depth, eolian sands at parent material, and occur on the meander scrolls and active dunes.
Atigaru soils have permafrost and occur on non-polygonal stream terraces.
Bodfish soils are somewhat poorly drained, have an irregular decrease in organic matter between the surface and subsurface horizons, and occurs on floodplains.
Polakoon soils are very poorly drained, have permafrost, have a histic epipedon, and occur on depressions near streams and in string bogs.
Tuluga soils have aquic conditions and occur on depressions on stream terraces.
