MILLENDER SERIES

MLRA(s)--152A
Depth class--Very deep
Drainage class--Very poorly drained
Parent material--Fine-loamy estuarine deposits
Geomorphic location--Oligohaline and mesohaline tidal marshes along tidal river floodplains 
Slope range--0 to 1 percent
Mean annual air temperature--21 degrees C (70 degrees F)
Mean annual precipitation--1397 millimeters (55 inches)

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Fine-loamy, mixed, active, nonacid, hyperthermic Sulfic Hydraquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Millender mucky loam on an oligohaline tidal marsh, 0.6 meters above sea level (colors are for moist soil unless otherwise stated; electrical conductivity is 1:1 by volume solution mixture unless otherwise stated).

 Aseg1--0 to 28 centimeters; dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) mucky loam; massive; nonsticky, nonplastic; very fluid; many very fine, common fine, and few coarse roots; slight sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderate effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; nonsaline (0.1 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.1), moderately acid (pH 5.7) oxidized pH; clear boundary

 Aseg2--28 to 64 centimeters; very dark grayish brown (2.5Y 3/2) mucky loam; massive; slightly sticky, nonplastic; very fluid; common very fine and few fine roots; moderate sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; nonsaline (1.4 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.0), very strongly acid (pH 4.7) oxidized pH; clear boundary (50 to 80 centimeters thick)

 Oaseb--64 to 101 centimeters; very dark gray (10YR 3/1) broken face and very dark brown (10YR 2/2) rubbed muck; 15 percent fibers, 3 percent rubbed; von Post H8; moderate sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderate effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; very slightly saline (2.3 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.1), extremely acid (pH 3.8) oxidized pH; clear boundary (18 to 54 centimeters thick)

 Cseg1--101 to 154 centimeters; dark gray (10YR 4/1) clay loam; massive; moderately sticky, moderately plastic; very fluid; 10 percent wood fragments; moderate sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; very slightly saline (3.8 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.3), extremely acid (pH 4.2) oxidized pH; gradual boundary

 Cseg2--154 to 200 centimeters; dark gray (2.5Y 4/1) clay loam; massive; moderately sticky, slightly plastic; very fluid; moderate sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderate effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; slightly saline (4.3 dS/m); slightly alkaline (pH 7.4), very strongly acid (pH 4.8) oxidized pH

TYPE LOCATION: Franklin County, Florida; St. Marks River; approximately 2.4 kilometers (1.5 miles) north of the intersection of Water Street and G Avenue, and approximately 7.2 kilometers (4.5 miles) south of Gardener Landing; USGS topographic quadrangle: Beverly

Frost free period--300 to 350 days

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Blackrail, Cottonmouth, Fushatchee, and Haulover soils.
Blackrail soils--Formed in sandy alluvium and estuarine deposits on similar landforms.
Cottonmouth soils--Formed in herbaceous organic materials over loamy estuarine deposits and have greater than 40 centimeters of hemic soil material at the surface.
Fushatchee soils--Formed in herbaceous organic materials over loamy estuarine deposits and have greater than 40 centimeters of sapric soil material at the surface.
Haulover soils--Formed in fine-loamy estuarine deposits, contain sulfidic materials within 50 centimeters of the soil surface, and have histic epipedons.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:
Drainage class--Very poorly drained
Saturated hydraulic conductivity--Moderately high to high
Soil moisture regime--Peraquic
Flooding--Very frequent, for brief durations. Millender soils are flooded by wind and lunar tides, or overbank flow. Inundation depths on these soils are irregular.

USE AND VEGETATION:
Major uses--Millender soils are used mainly as wetland wildlife habitat.
Typical vegetation--Black needlerush (Juncus roemerianus), sawgrass (Cladium jamaicense), hardstem bullrush (Schoenoplectus acutus), bulltongue arrowhead (Sagittaria lancifolia), and cosmopolitan bullrush (Bolboschoenus maritimus).

REMARKS:
The series is named after Millender Road, near where it was observed. Millender soils were previously mapped as the Bayvi, Bohicket, Brickyard, Chowan, Dirego, Kenner, and Tisonia soil series in Florida soil surveys.

Diagnostic horizons and other diagnostic soil characteristics recognized in this pedon are (Keys to Soil Taxonomy, Thirteenth Edition, 2022):
Ochric epipedon--The zone from 0 to 64 centimeters
Fluidity--The zone from 0 to 64 and 101 to 200 centimeters has a fluidity class of very fluid.
Sapric soil materials--The zone from 64 to 101 centimeters has a fiber content after rubbing that is less than one-sixth of the original soil volume.
Hyposulfidic materials--The zone from 0 to 64 centimeters and 101 to 200 centimeters has an initial pH that decreases by 1 or more units but remains at a value greater than 4.0 after 16 weeks of moist aerobic incubation.
Hypersulfidic materials--The zone from 64 to 101 centimeters has an initial pH that decreases by 1 or more units and decreases to a value of 4.0 or less after 16 weeks of moist aerobic incubation.
Peraquic feature--Zero or positive water potential at or near the soil surface.
