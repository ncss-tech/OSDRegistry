GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Piggsville, Tosa, and Vieau series which all occur on anthropogenic landforms with over 50 centimeters of human-transported material.

Piggsville soils formed in loamy-skeletal human-transported material; occur on fills on nearby ground moraines and escarpments.
Vieau soils formed in sandy dredge spoils; occur on dredge spoil banks on nearby beach terraces.
Tosa soils have aquic conditions between 30 and 60 centimeters and occur on fills on nearby fills on ground moraines.
