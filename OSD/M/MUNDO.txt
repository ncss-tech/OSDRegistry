Depth class: Very deep

Parent material: Lacustrine deposits or alluvium derived from mixed rock sources
Geomorphic location: Playas and basin floors
Slope range: 0 to 2 percent

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Fine-loamy over clayey, mixed over smectitic, superactive, hyperthermic Typic Aquisalids

TYPICAL PEDON: Mundo sandy loam on a nearly level playa. When described the soil was moist throughout. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 Cnsezg--0 to 10 centimeters; olive gray (5Y 5/2), moist; sandy loam; moderate medium platy structure; slightly hard, friable, slightly sticky, slightly plastic,; nonfluid; common medium irregular and fine interstitial pores; 5 percent medium distinct irregular strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) masses of oxidized iron and 5 percent medium prominent irregular gray (2.5Y 5/1) iron depletions; 5 percent medium prominent platy noncoherent salt masses with sharp boundaries along lamina or strata surfaces; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnseg1--10 to 60 centimeters; dark greenish gray (10Y 4/1), moist; silty clay loam; massive; hard, friable, very sticky, very plastic, moderately fluid; 20 percent coarse distinct irregular very dark gray (N 3/) masses of reduced iron monosulfide; slight sulfurous odor; positive color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); abrupt smooth boundary.

 Cnseg2--60 to 85 centimeters; dark gray (7.5YR 4/1), moist; stratified fine sandy loam to silty clay loam; massive; hard, friable, very sticky, very plastic, moderately fluid; slight sulfurous odor; positive color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slightly effervescent (30 percent hydrogen peroxide); strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.7); abrupt smooth boundary.

 2Ckn--85 to 200 centimeters; dark reddish brown (5YR 3/2) and reddish brown (5YR 4/4), moist; clay; massive; extremely hard, very firm, very sticky, very plastic, slightly fluid; common very fine tubular pores; common distinct pressure faces on all faces of peds; very few medium prominent threadlike noncoherent cemented white (10YR 8/1) carbonate masses; violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.6).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Calipatria, Imperial, Niland, Saltisun, Slabcity and Wonderstone soils.
Calipatria soils have less than 18 percent clay in the upper part of the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Imperial soils do not have salic horizons, are saturated in the soil moisture control section for less than 30 days, and occur on higher terraces of lakebeds.
Niland soils do not have salic horizons, have sandy textures in the upper part of the particle-size control section, and occur on adjacent floodplains.
Saltisun soils have less than 18 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Slabcity soils have greater than 35 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Wonderstone soils have 18 to 35 percent clay throughout the particle-size control section and are on adjacent playas.
