GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Calipatria, Glenbar, Mundo, Naas, Saltisun, and Salton soils.

Calipatria soils have less than 18 percent clay in the upper part of the particle-size control section, are formed in mixed over smectitic parent material, and occur on adjacent playas.
Glenbar soils have an aridic soil moisture regime and occur on flood plains and alluvial fans.
Naas soils have sandy textures in the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas and beach ridges.
Mundo soils are clayey in the lower part of the particle-size control section, have a mixed over smectitic mineralogy class, and occur on adjacent playas.
Saltisun soils have less than 18 percent clay in the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Salton soils do not have a salic horizon, have less than 15 percent sand coarser than very fine sand in the particle-size control section, and occur on slightly higher positions of the lacustrine basin.
Slabcity soils have greater than 35 percent clay in the particle-size control section and are on adjacent playas.
