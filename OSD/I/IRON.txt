IRON SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: sandy and gravelly alluvium 
Landscape: valleys 
Landform: flood plains 
Slope: 0 to 3 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (74 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C. (37 degrees F.) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy-skeletal, mixed Typic Cryorthents

TYPICAL PEDON: Iron silt loam in a mixed spruce-poplar forest on a north facing convex, convex on a rise on a slope of 5 percent at an elevation of 54m (177 ft).

     Oe--0 to 5 cm (0 to 2 in); moderately decomposed plant material, black (10YR 2/1) rubbed moist, dark gray (10YR 4/1) dry; many fine roots, common very fine roots and few medium roots throughout; moderately acid (5.6 pH); clear smooth boundary.

     A--5 to 8 cm (2 to 3 in); silt loam, very dark gray (7.5YR 3/1) broken face moist, gray (7.5YR 5/1) dry; weak fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common very fine and fine roots and few medium and coarse roots throughout; 5 percent subrounded mixed gravel by volume; moderately acid (5.8 pH); clear wavy boundary.

     C--8 to 200 cm (3 to 79 in); extremely gravelly sand, very dark gray (7.5YR 3/1) broken face moist, gray (7.5YR 5/1) dry; single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; few fine roots at the top of the horizon; 10 percent subrounded mixed cobbles by volume, 60 percent subrounded mixed gravel by volume; neutral (6.8 pH).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.1689878, longitude -149.5032626, datum WGS84.
