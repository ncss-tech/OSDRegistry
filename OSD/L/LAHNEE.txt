LAHNEE SERIES

MLRA(s)--152A
Depth class--Very deep
Drainage class--Very poorly drained
Parent material--Fine-loamy lagoonal deposits
Geomorphic location--Lagoonal and barrier island tidal marshes
Slope range--0 to 1 percent
Mean annual air temperature--17 degrees C (63 degrees F)
Mean annual precipitation--1270 millimeters (50 inches)

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Fine-loamy, mixed, active, nonacid, hyperthermic Sodic Hydraquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Lahnee loam in a polyhaline marsh at about sea level (colors are for moist soil unless otherwise stated; electrical conductivity is 1:1 by volume solution mixture unless otherwise stated).

 Aseg--0 to 24 centimeters; 60 percent greenish gray (10Y 5/1) and 40 percent black (N 2.5/) loam; massive; slightly sticky, nonplastic; very fluid; common very fine, fine, and medium roots; slight sulfurous odor; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderate effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; strongly saline (17.1 dS/m); slightly acid (pH 6.4), strongly acid (pH 5.5) oxidized pH; clear boundary (10 to 40 centimeters thick)

 Cseg1--24 to 42 centimeters; greenish gray (10Y 5/1) loam; massive; moderately sticky, slightly plastic; very fluid; few very fine and medium roots, common fine roots; no sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; strongly saline (16.5 dS/m); neutral (pH 6.9), neutral (pH 6.7) oxidized pH

 Cseg2--42 to 73 centimeters; very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1) sandy loam; massive; slightly sticky, nonplastic; very fluid; few very fine and fine roots; slight sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderate effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderately saline (14.3 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.0), neutral (pH 7.3) oxidized pH; clear boundary

 Cseg3--73 to 200 centimeters; 60 percent dark greenish gray (10Y 4/1) and 40 percent dark greenish gray (10Y 3/1) sandy clay loam; massive; moderately sticky, nonplastic; very fluid; few very fine and fine roots at the top of the horizon; slight sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; moderately saline (12.7 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.1), neutral (pH 7.2) oxidized pH

TYPE LOCATION: Franklin County, Forida; St. Vincent National Wildlife Refuge; approximately 0.4 kilometers (0.2 miles) south of the intersection of I Road and 5 Road on St. Vincent National Wildlife Refuge, and approximately 9.4 kilometers (5.8 miles) east of Indian Pass; USGS topographic quadrangle: Indian Pass.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Decuevas, Hacokewa, Hoyana, and Pompano soils.
Hacokewa soils--formed in herbaceous organic material and have greater than 40 centimeters of soil organic material.
Hoyana soils--formed in coarse-loamy lagoonal materials on similar landforms.
Pompano soils--formed in thick beds of sandy marine sediments, are poorly drained, and are often associated with landforms away from the coast.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:
Drainage class--Very poorly drained
Saturated hydraulic conductivity--Moderately high to high
Soil moisture regime--Peraquic
Flooding--Very frequent, for very brief durations. Lahnee soils are flooded by lunar tides.

USE AND VEGETATION:
Major uses--Lahnee soils are used mainly as wetland wildlife habitat.
Typical vegetation--Black needlerush (Juncus roemerianus), smooth cordgrass (Spartina alterniflora), saltgrass (Distichlis spicata), and Sea purslane (Sesuvium portulacastrum). Black needlerush on Lahnee soils typically exhibits high salinity stress and is less than one meter tall.

REMARKS:
The series is named after the anglicized spelling of the Muscogee Creek word for "green". Lahnee soils were previously mapped as the Bayvi, Bohicket, Dirego, and Tisonia soil series in Florida soil surveys.

Diagnostic horizons and other diagnostic soil characteristics recognized in this pedon are (Keys to Soil Taxonomy, Thirteenth Edition, 2022):
Ochric epipedon--The zone from 0 to 24 centimeters.
Hyposulfidic materials--The zone from 0 to 24 centimeters has an initial pH that decreases by 1 or more units but remains at a value of 4.0 or more after 16 weeks of moist aerobic incubation.
Fluidity--The zone from 0 to 200 centimeters has a fluidity class of very fluid.
Peraquic feature--Zero or positive water potential at or near the soil surface.
