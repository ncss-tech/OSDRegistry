EXIT SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: loamy-skeletal colluvium 
Landscape: mountains 
Landform: mountain slopes 
Slope: 20 to 90 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 2380 mm (94 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.) 
Frost-free period: 60 to 100 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy-skeletal, mixed, superactive Typic Haplocryods

TYPICAL PEDON: Exit very channery silt loam in an alder community on an east facing linear, convex backslope on a slope of 65 percent at an elevation of 384m (1260 ft).

     Oi--0 to 10 cm (0 to 4 in); slightly decomposed plant material, dark reddish brown (5YR 2.5/2) moist, dark reddish gray (5YR 4/2) dry; many very fine & fine roots throughout; extremely acid (pH 3.7); abrupt wavy boundary.

     A--10 to 20 cm (4 to 8 in); very channery silt loam, black (10YR 2/1) moist, dark gray (10YR 4/1) dry; weak medium granular structure; slightly sticky, nonplastic; many very fine and common medium and fine roots throughout; 38 percent angular sedimentary channers by volume; extremely acid (pH 3.7); clear wavy boundary.

     AE--20 to 49 cm (8 to 19 in); very channery silt loam, grayish brown (10YR 5/2) moist, light gray (10YR 7/2) dry; weak fine subangular blocky structure; slightly sticky, nonplastic; many very fine and fine and common medium roots throughout; 45 percent angular sedimentary channers by volume; extremely acid (pH 3.7); gradual wavy boundary.

     Bhs--49 to 62 cm (19 to 24 in); very channery silt loam, dark reddish brown (5YR 3/3) moist, reddish brown (5YR 5/3) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; slightly sticky, nonplastic; many very fine and fine roots and common medium and coarse roots throughout; 50 percent angular sedimentary channers by volume; extremely acid (pH 4.3); gradual wavy boundary.

     Bs--62 to 104 cm (24 to 41 in); very channery silt loam, dark reddish brown (5YR 3/4) moist, reddish brown (5YR 5/4) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; slightly sticky, nonplastic; common very fine and fine roots throughout; 55 percent angular sedimentary channers by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.9); gradual wavy boundary.

     C--104 to 200 cm (41 to 79 in); extremely channery silt loam, reddish brown (5YR 4/4) moist, light reddish brown (5YR 6/4) dry; massive; common medium and fine roots throughout; 65 percent angular sedimentary channers by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.9).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.1169444, longitude -149.4625000, datum WGS84.

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Deception (AK), Homestead (AK), Remedios (AK), and Waitabit (AK) series. 
Deception soils lack a Bhs horizon and have a lithologic discontinuity.

Remedios soils lack an albic horizon.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Elevation: 200 to 900 meters (657 to 2953 feet) 
Parent material: loamy-skeletal volcanic ash and/or loamy-skeletal colluvium 
Landform: mountain slopes

Mean annual precipitation: 2090 to 2800 mm (82 to 110 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: 0 to 3 degrees C. (32 to 37 degrees F.)

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: This is the Ski soil. 
Ski soils have a lithic contact between 50 to 100 cm (20 to 39 in), have andic soil properties, have aquic conditions within 15 cm (6 in) and occur on upper thirds of mountainflanks.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:  
Drainage class: well 
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: very high of Oi horizon, moderately high to high in the A, AE, Bhs, and Bs horizons, and moderately high to very high in the C horizon.
Permeability: very rapid in the Oi horizon, moderate in the A and AE horizons, moderate to moderately rapid in the Bhs and Bs horizons, and moderate to very rapid in the C horizon.
Runoff: high
