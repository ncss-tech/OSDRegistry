HUMPY SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: very poorly drained, poorly drained 
Parent material: coarse-loamy alluvium 
Landscape: valleys 
Landform: depressions, flood plains 
Slope: 0 to 4 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (73 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37 F) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy, mixed, superactive, nonacid Typic Cryaquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Humpy silt in a maritime forest loamy wet plains community on a north facing concave, linear floodplain on a slope of 3 percent at an elevation of 152 m (499 ft).

     Oe--0 to 8 cm (0 to 3 in); mucky peat, dark brown (10YR 3/3) rubbed moist, brown (10YR 5/3) rubbed dry; common very fine and fine roots throughout; strongly acid (pH 5.3), clear smooth boundary.

     Cg1--8 to 16 cm (3 to 6 in); silt, dark gray (2.5Y 4/1) rubbed moist, gray (2.5Y 6/1) rubbed dry; massive; soft, very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common very fine and fine roots throughout; interstitial pores; reduced matrix; slightly acid (pH 6.5), clear wavy boundary.

     Cg2--16 to 30 cm (6 to 12 in); silt, dark gray (2.5Y 4/1) rubbed moist, gray (2.5Y 6/1) rubbed dry; massive; soft, very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; interstitial pores; reduced matrix; slightly acid (pH 6.5), clear wavy boundary.

     Cg3--30 to 200 cm (12 to 79 in); silt loam, dark gray (2.5Y 4/1) rubbed moist, gray (2.5Y 6/1) rubbed dry;single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; interstitial pores; reduced matrix; neutral (pH 6.8).

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Aquatna (AK), Beluga (AK), Excursion (AK), Moose River (AK), Noonku (AK), Ottercreek (AK), Susivar (AK), Thirdlake (AK) series. 
Aquatna soils occur at elevation ranges from 716 to 762 m (2350 to 2500 ft) and have average annual precipitation of 406 mm (16 in). 
Beluga soils have a mean annual precipitation of 610 mm (24 in), a firm underlying material, and slopes ranging from 0 to 15 percent. 
Excursion soils have neutral to moderately alkaline mineral material and a histic epipedon. 
Moose River soils have a mean annual precipitation of 356 to 711 mm (14 to 28 in) and a sandy substratum. 
Noonku soils occur on channels and sloughs on flood plains and have a mean annual precipitation of 305 mm (12 in). 
Ottercreek soils occur on mountain slope landforms, have slopes ranging from 3 to 20 percent, and have mean annual precipitations of 595 mm (23 in). 
Susivar soils are somewhat poorly drained. 
Thirdlake soils have a mean annual air temperature of -3 C (27 F) and rock fragments throughout the profile.
