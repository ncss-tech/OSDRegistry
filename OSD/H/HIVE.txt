HIVE SERIES

Depth class: very deep
Drainage class: very poorly drained
Parent material: organic material
Landscape: hills
Landform: depressions, hillslopes
Slope: 0 to 10 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (74 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C. (37 degrees F.)
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Euic Typic Cryohemists

TYPICAL PEDON: Hive peat in a sedge scrub meadow community on a west southwest facing linear, concave toeslope on a slope of 1 percent at an elevation of 224m (735 ft).

     Oi--0 to 22 cm (0 to 9 in); peat, dark brown (7.5YR 3/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 5/2) dry; common medium and fine roots throughout; strongly acid (pH 5.3); diffuse smooth boundary.

     Oe--22 to 200 cm (9 to 79 in); mucky peat, dark brown (10YR 3/3) moist, brown (10YR 5/3) dry;  moderately acid (pH 5.7).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.2537210, longitude -149.3577768, datum WGS84

RANGE IN CHARACTERISTICS:

Soil moisture regime: aquic
Soil temperature regime: cryic
Mean annual soil temperature: 0 to 4 degrees C. (32 to 39 degrees F.)
Thickness of organic material: 200 cm (79 in)
Thickness of fibric soil materials: 0 to 35 cm (0 to 14 in)
Thickness of hemic soil materials: 165 to 200 cm (65 to 79 in)
Depth to aquic conditions: 0 cm (0 in)

Oi horizon:
Hue: 7.5YR, 10YR or 2.5Y
Value: 3 to 6 moist, 5 to 7 dry
Chroma: 2 to 4

Reaction class: very strongly acid to slightly acid (pH 4.5 to 6.4)

Oe horizon:
Hue: 7.5YR or 10YR
Value: 2 to 3 moist, 4 or 5 dry
Chroma: 1 to 3
Organic matter: 60 to 90 percent
Reaction class: very strongly acid to slightly acid (pH 4.5 to 6.4)

Some pedons have a sandy-skeletal substratum at depths greater than 170 cm (67 in).

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Alitak (AK), Ekiligamut (AK), Graylake (AK), Johnsonriver (AK), Koyuktolik (AK), Mariel (ID, MT, OR), Octopuslake (AK), Snakeriver (AK), and Wocus (OR) series.
Alitak soils lack a pH ranging to greater than 6.0 have a mean annual precipitation of 600 to 2000 mm (23 to 79 in).
Ekiligamut soils have silty marine deposits in the substratum.
Graylake soils have a pH range from 6.2 to 7.8 and have a mean annual precipitation less than 550 mm (22 in).
Johnsonriver soils have coarse-loamy alluvium in the substratum and have a mean annual precipitation of 400 to 600 mm (16 to 24 in).
Koyuktolik soils have discontinuous strata of volcanic ash throughout the organic soil horizons and have glacial till in the substratum.
Mariel soils have an Oa horizon in the substratum and have a mean annual precipitation of 1270 to 2900 mm (50 to 114 in).
Octopuslake soils lack fibric soil materials and have a mean annual precipitation of 300 to 800 mm (12 to 31 in).
Snakeriver soils have a pH range from 5.0 to 6.5 and have a mean annual precipitation of 580 to 2000 mm (23 to 79 in).
Wocus soils have diatomaceous earth in the substratum.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Andarcher, Moose, and Porcupineglacier, soils.
Andarcher soils have a paralithic contact at depths between 100 to 150 cm (39 to 59 in), have an albic horizon, have a spodic horizon, have andic soil properties and occur on backslopes.
Moose soils have a paralithic contact at depths between 50 to 100 cm (20 to 39 in), have an albic horizon, have a spodic horizon, have andic soil properties and occur on backslopes, shoulders and toeslopes.
Porcupineglacier soils have a lithic contact at depths between 25 to 50 cm (10 to 20 in), have an albic horizon, have a spodic horizon, have andic soil properties and occur on backslopes.
