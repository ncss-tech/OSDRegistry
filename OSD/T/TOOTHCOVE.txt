TOOTHCOVE SERIES

Depth class: very deep
Drainage class: well drained
Parent material: loamy-skeletal alluvium
Landscape: valleys
Landform: terraces
Slope: 3 to 7 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (73 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37 F)
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy-skeletal, mixed, superactive Typic Humicryods

TYPICAL PEDON: Toothcove highly organic silt loam in a Sitka spruce community on a linear, convex terrace on a slope of 5 percent at an elevation of 50m (164 ft).

     A--0 to 5 cm (0 to 2 in); highly organic silt loam, dark brown (7.5YR 3/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 5/2) dry; moderate fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common very fine and medium roots throughout and many fine roots; 7 percent rounded gravels by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.8), abrupt wavy boundary.

     E--5 to 9 cm (2 to 4 in); very fine sandy loam, dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) moist, light brownish gray (10YR 6/2) dry; moderate medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common very fine, medium, and coarse roots and many fine roots; 7 percent rounded gravels by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.8); abrupt broken boundary.

     Bhs--9 to 14 cm (4 to 6 in); highly organic silt loam, black (5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (5YR 4/1) dry; weak fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many very fine and fine roots and common medium and coarse roots; 10 percent rounded gravels by volume; very strongly acid (pH 5.0); abrupt wavy boundary.

     E'--14 to 27 cm (6 to 11 in); very gravelly silt loam, light gray (2.5Y 7/2) moist, pale yellow (2.5Y 8/2) dry; strong medium subangular blocky structure; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine and medium roots; 37 percent rounded gravels by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.1); abrupt wavy boundary.

     B'hs--27 to 45 cm (11 to 18 in); extremely gravelly highly organic silt loam, very dusky red (2.5YR 2.5/2) moist, weak red (2.5YR 4/2) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common very fine, fine, and medium roots; 70 percent rounded gravels by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.3); clear wavy boundary.

     Bs--45 to 77 cm (18 to 30 in); extremely gravelly sandy loam, 70 percent brown (7.5YR 4/4) and 30 percent yellowish red (5YR 4/6) moist, 70 percent light brown (7.5YR 6/4) and 30 percent reddish yellow (5YR 6/6) dry; weak medium subangular blocky structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine and medium roots; 80 percent rounded gravels by volume; strongly acid (pH 5.4); clear wavy boundary.

     C--77 to 200 cm (30 to 79 in); extremely gravelly loamy sand, very dark grayish brown (2.5Y 3/2) moist, grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) dry; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; 85 percent rounded gravels by volume; moderately acid (pH 5.6).
