GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Milwaukee and Piggsville series which are also formed in human-transported material on anthropogenic landforms.

Milwaukee soils are deeper than 100 cm to aquic conditions and occur on fills on nearby ground moraines.
Piggsville soils formed in loamy-skeletal human-transported material; occur on steeper areas of floodways in drainageways.
