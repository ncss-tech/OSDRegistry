GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Inigok, and Pingok series.
Anajuk soils are very poorly drained, have permafrost, have cryoturbated and glacic horizons, and occur on low relief troughs of high-center polygons.
Inigok soils are poorly drained, have permafrost, have cryoturbation, and occur on incised streams and patterned ground.
Pingok soils are very poorly drained, are organic soils, have permafrost and a glacic layer, and occur on high centered polygons.
