TYPICAL PEDON: Vieau fine sandy loam, 4 percent slope on a dredge spoil bank at an elevation of 184 meters above mean sea level. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 ^Au--0 to 18 centimeters (0 to 7 inches); 98 percent very dark gray (10YR 3/1) and 2 percent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6); fine sandy loam; weak fine granular structure; friable; many very fine and fine roots throughout; many very fine and fine irregular pores; 2 percent mixed gravel; 5 percent gravel sized coal combustion byproducts; slightly effervescent neutral (pH 7.0); abrupt smooth boundary (0 to 20 cm thick)

 ^Bw--18 to 38 centimeters (7 to 15 inches); 87 percent brown (7.5YR 4/4), 6 percent very dark gray (10YR 3/1), 4 percent reddish brown (5YR 4/4), and 3 percent pinkish gray (7.5YR 7/2) loamy coarse sand; weak fine subangular blocky structure; friable; common very fine and fine roots throughout; many very fine and fine irregular pores; 6 percent mixed fine gravel and 2 percent mixed coarse gravel; slightly effervescent; moderately alkaline (pH 8.2); clear smooth boundary (20 to 40 cm thick)

 ^C1--38 to 58 centimeters (15 to 23 inches); 97 percent brown (7.5YR 5/3) and 3 percent strong brown (7.5YR 5/6) loamy fine sand; single grain; loose; 2 percent mixed gravel; slightly effervescent with 1 N Hydrochloric acid; moderately alkaline (pH 8.2); gradual smooth boundary 

 ^C2--58 to 81 centimeters (23 to 32 inches); 91 percent brown (7.5YR 5/3) and 7 percent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) and 2 percent pinkish gray (7.5YR 7/2) loamy sand; single grain; loose; 3 percent mixed gravel; slightly effervescent; moderately alkaline (pH 8.4); gradual smooth boundary

 ^C3--81 to 114 centimeters (32 to 45 inches); 93 percent brown (10YR 5/3) and 4 percent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) and 3 percent very dark gray (7.5YR 3/1) loamy sand; single grain; loose; 7 percent mixed gravel; strongly effervescent; moderately alkaline (pH 8.4); gradual smooth boundary

 ^C4--114 to 137 centimeters (45 to 54 inches); 90 percent yellowish brown (10YR 5/4) and 10 percent strong brown (7.5YR 4/6) gravelly sand; single grain; loose; 2 percent mixed fine gravel, 3 percent mixed medium gravel and 10 percent mixed coarse gravel; strongly effervescent; strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); gradual smooth boundary 

 ^C5--137 to 168 centimeters (54 to 66 inches); 82 percent brown (7.5YR 4/4) and 16 percent yellowish brown (10YR 5/4) and 2 percent dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/6) gravelly coarse sand; single grain; loose; 3 percent mixed fine gravel, 5 percent mixed medium gravel and 12 percent mixed coarse gravel; strongly effervescent; strongly alkaline (pH 8.5); gradual smooth boundary (Combined thickness of the ^C horizons 50 to 160 centimeters)

 2C--168 to 200 centimeters (66 to 79 inches); yellowish brown (10YR 5/4) gravelly coarse sand; single grain; loose; 2 fine mixed gravel, 5 percent mixed medium gravel and 10 percent mixed coarse gravel; strongly effervescent; strongly alkaline (pH 8.5)

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Piggsville, Milwaukee, and Tosa series.

Milwaukee soils formed in silty human-transported material; occur on fills on nearby ground moraines.
Piggsville soils formed in loamy-skeletal human-transported material; occur on fills on nearby ground moraines and escarpments.
Tosa soils have loamy till below the human-transported material and have aquic conditions; occur on fills on nearby ground moraines.
