TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy, mixed, hyperthermic, shallow Gypsic Aquisalids

TYPICAL PEDON: Frink extremely shelly sand on a nearly level playa in an area with extensive surface heaving of petrogypsic material at an elevation of -73 meters. (Colors are for moist soil unless otherwise noted.)

 Cn--0 to 14 centimeters; light yellowish brown (2.5Y 6/3), light gray (2.5Y 7/2) dry; extremely shelly sand; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine roots; 60 percent weakly coherent subangular shell fragments; violently effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 8.2); abrupt smooth boundary. (5 to 30 centimeters thick)

 Bnym--14 to 21 centimeters; olive (5Y 5/3), light olive gray (5Y 6/2), dry; strongly coherent material, cemented by gypsum; rigid; common fine roots; 10 percent medium prominent white (10YR 8/1) carbonate masses along strata; 10 percent subangular shell fragments; moderately alkaline (pH 8.2); abrupt smooth boundary. (2 to 15 centimeters thick)

 Cng1--21 to 44 centimeters; 80 percent gray (2.5Y 5/1) and 20 percent black (2.5Y 2.5/1), 80 percent gray (2.5Y 6/1) and 20 percent very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1), dry; sand; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine roots; 10 percent coarse distinct noncoherent dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/4) masses of oxidized iron; 2 percent subangular shell fragments; strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 8.1); abrupt wavy boundary.

 Cng2--44 to 65 centimeters; 80 percent dark gray (2.5Y 4/1) and 20 percent gray (2.5Y 5/1); loamy fine sand; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic, nonfluid; common fine roots; 10 percent medium prominent dark yellowish brown (10YR 3/4) masses of oxidized iron; strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); abrupt wavy boundary. (Combined thickness of Cng horizons is 0 to 190 centimeters)

 Cnseg--65 to 116 centimeters; dark gray (2.5Y 4/1); stratified coarse sand and loamy sand; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; 5 percent subangular shell fragments; color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; strongly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 8.2); abrupt wavy boundary. (5 to 190 centimeters thick)

 C'ng--116 to 160 centimeters; gray (2.5Y 5/1), moist; stratified loamy sand and sandy loam; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; 2 percent diorite fragments and 5 percent subangular shell fragments; slightly effervescent (1 normal hydrochloric acid); moderately alkaline (pH 7.9)

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Calipatria, Corvina, Mundo, Niland, Saltisun, and Wonderstone soils, none of which have petrogypsic horizons.
Calipatria soils have less than 75 percent sand in the upper part of the particle-size control section, formed in mixed over smectitic mineralogy, and occur on adjacent playas.
Mundo soils have greater than 18 percent clay in the particle-size control section, formed in mixed over smectitic parent material, and occur on adjacent playas.
Corvina soils do not have a restrictive layer but have otherwise similar coarse textures and occur on adjacent playas. 
Niland soils do not have salic horizons, are saturated in the soil moisture control section for less than 30 days, and occur on adjacent floodplains.
Saltisun soils have less than 75 percent sand throughout the particle-size control section and occur on adjacent playas.
Wonderstone soils have 18 to 35 percent clay in the particle-size control section and are on adjacent playas.
