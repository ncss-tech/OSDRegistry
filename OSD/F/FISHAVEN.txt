FISHAVEN SERIES

The Fishaven series consists of moderately deep, well drained soils that formed in slope alluvium and colluvium over residuum weathered from limestone. These soils are on hillslopes. Slopes are 8 to 20 percent. Mean annual precipitation is about 425 mm, and mean annual air temperature is about 4 degrees C.

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy, carbonatic, frigid Typic Calcixerolls

TYPICAL PEDON: Fishaven gravelly loam in rangeland. (Colors are for dry soil unless otherwise noted.)

 A1--0 to 8 cm; dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) gravelly loam, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist; moderate very fine granular structure; soft, very friable, nonsticky and slightly plastic; many very fine roots; many very fine irregular pores; strongly effervescent (18 percent calcium carbonate equivalent); finely disseminated calcium carbonate; 20 percent gravel; slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); abrupt smooth boundary.

 A2--8 to 25 cm; dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) silt loam, very dark gray (10YR 3/1) moist; strong very fine and fine granular structure; soft, very friable, slightly sticky and slightly plastic; many very fine and few fine roots; many very fine and few fine tubular pores; strongly effervescent (25 percent calcium carbonate equivalent); finely disseminated calcium carbonate; 10 percent gravel; slightly alkaline (pH 7.8); clear wavy boundary.

 BA--25 to 41 cm; grayish brown (10YR 5/2) gravelly loam, very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2) moist; moderate fine and medium subangular blocky structure parting to moderate very fine granular; slightly hard, very friable, slightly sticky and slightly plastic; common very fine and medium roots; many very fine and few fine tubular pores; violently effervescent (40 percent calcium carbonate equivalent); finely disseminated calcium carbonate; 20 percent gravel; moderately alkaline (pH 8.0); clear wavy boundary.

 Bk--41 to 56 cm; light gray (10YR 7/2) gravelly loam, grayish brown (10YR 5/2) moist; moderate fine and medium subangular blocky structure; slightly hard, friable, slightly sticky and slightly plastic; common very fine roots; many very fine tubular pores; violently effervescent (55 percent calcium carbonate equivalent); finely disseminated calcium carbonate in the matrix and calcium carbonate coats on bottom sides of rock fragments; 20 percent gravel; moderately alkaline (pH 8.2); clear wavy boundary.

 C--56 to 69 cm; very pale brown (10YR 8/2) very gravelly loam, pale brown (10YR 6/3) moist; massive; hard, friable, nonsticky and slightly plastic; common very fine roots; common very fine tubular pores; violently effervescent (greater than 65 percent calcium carbonate equivalent); finely disseminated calcium carbonate; 40 percent gravel; moderately alkaline (pH 8.4); abrupt wavy boundary.

 R--69 cm; limestone.

TYPE LOCATION: Bear Lake County, Idaho; USGS Saint Charles topographic quadrangle; latitude 42.0033333 degrees north, longitude  -111.4199722 degrees west, datum WGS84.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING:
Landform--hillslopes
Elevation--1,790 to 2,010 meters
Slope--8 to 20 percent
Parent material--alluvium, slope alluvium, colluvium, and residuum dominantly from limestone
Mean annual precipitation--350 to 500 mm
Mean annual air temperature--2.2 to 6.0 degrees C
Frost-free period--60 to 90 days

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: None listed.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: Well drained, 5 to 15 um/sec

USE AND VEGETATION: Fishaven soils are primarily used for building sites, nonirrigated cropland and rangeland. The potential native vegetation is mountain big sagebrush and bluebunch wheatgrass.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: Fishaven soils are of small extent in southeastern Idaho; MLRAs 13 and 47.

SOIL SURVEY REGIONAL OFFICE (SSRO) RESPONSIBLE: Bozeman, Montana

SERIES ESTABLISHED: Bear Lake County, Idaho, 2008. The name is taken from a nearby community.

REMARKS: Diagnostic horizons and features recognized in this pedon:
Mollic epipedon--0 to 41 cm (A1, A2 and BA horizons)
Calcic horizon--41 to 56 cm (Bk horizon)
Lithic contact--69 cm (R horizon)

Particle-size control section--25 to 69 cm (BA, Bk and C horizons)

Fishaven soils have a frigid temperature regime and a xeric moisture regime.
