FONTSPOINT SERIES

The Fontspoint series consists of shallow, well drained soils that formed in residuum from siltstone and mudstone. Fontspoint soils are on hills in the badlands and have slopes of 0 to 50 percent. The mean annual precipitation is about 110 mm, and the mean annual temperature is about 22 degrees C.

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy, mixed, superactive, calcareous, hyperthermic, shallow Typic Torriorthents

TYPICAL PEDON: Fontspoint silty clay loam on a slope of 24 percent under barren land cover with 5 percent gravel surface fragments. (Colors are for dry soil unless otherwise noted.)

  Ayz--0 to 6 cm; grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) silty clay loam, dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) moist; moderate coarse subangular blocky structure; friable, slightly hard, very sticky, very plastic; salt, finely disseminated; 1 percent fine distinct threadlike weakly coherent cemented gypsum crystals in matrix;  2 percent medium distinct irregular noncoherent gypsum masses in matrix; 1 percent by volume fine mudstone paragravel; slight effervescence; strongly alkaline (pH 8.6); clear wavy boundary. (4 to 10 cm thick)

  Cyz1--6 to 16 cm; grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) silty clay loam, grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) moist; massive; very friable, very sticky, very plastic; salt, finely disseminated; 8 percent medium distinct irregular noncoherent gypsum masses in matrix; 2 percent fine mudstone paragravel; noneffervescent; strongly alkaline (pH 9.0); abrupt smooth boundary. (8 to 15 cm thick)

  Cyz2--16 to 35 cm; grayish brown (2.5Y 5/2) silt loam, dark grayish brown (2.5Y 4/2) moist; massive; very friable, soft, very sticky, very plastic; salt, finely disseminated; 25 percent medium distinct irregular noncoherent gypsum masses in matrix; 1 percent fine mudstone paragravel; noneffervescent; strongly alkaline (pH 8.7); clear wavy boundary. (16 to 24 cm thick)

  Cr--35 to 60 cm; massive; weakly coherent weathered mudstone

TYPE LOCATION: San Diego County, California; approximately 5.8 kilometers (3.6 miles) southwest on recreational route Fish Creek Wash starting from the intersection of Split Mountain Road and approximately 150 meters (500 feet) due south. USGS 7.5 minute topographic quadrangle: Carrizo Mountain NE, California. WGS84 32.9884800 latitude and -116.1189900 longitude; UTM Zone 11, 3650355 meters N and 582312 meters E WGS84.

RANGE IN CHARACTERISTICS:
Soil Temperature: Hyperthermic temperature regime. Mean annual soil temperature at 50 cm depth is 22 degrees C, and the difference between mean summer and mean winter soil temperature is greater than 6 degrees C.

Soil Moisture: Typic-aridic soil moisture regime.  Usually dry, moist in some parts for short periods during winter and early spring and for 10 to 20 days cumulative between July and September following summer convection storms.

COMPETING SERIES:
These are the Beeline soils:
Beeline soils are very shallow (10 to 25 cm) to a paralithic contact with conglomerate and do not have secondary accumulations of carbonates, salts, or gypsum.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Buzzardsprings, Carsitas, Carrizo, Goldrose, Cahuilla, Rositas, Rizzo, and Vint soils:
Buzzardsprings are somewhat excessively drained, have a sandy particle- size control section, and are found on fan remnants.
Carsitas soils are somewhat excessively drained, are sandy throughout, and are found on alluvial fans, fan aprons, and in drainageways.
Carrizo: are excessively drained, have a sandy-skeletal particle-size control section, and are found on floodplains, fan piedmonts, and bolson floors.
Goldrose soils are somewhat excessively drained, have a sandy particle-size control section, and are found on alluvial fans.
Ocotillo soils have an argillic horizon, and are found on fan remnants.
Rositas soils are somewhat excessively drained, are sandy throughout, and found on dunes and sand sheets.
Rizzo soils are excessively drained, have a sandy-skeletal particle-size control section, and are found on alluvial fans, fan aprons, fan remnants, and drainageways.
Vint soils are somewhat excessively drained, have a sandy particle-size control section, and are found on flood plains.

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: Somewhat excessively to well drained; moderately high saturated hydraulic conductivity

USE AND VEGETATION: Used for wildlife and recreation. In general, there is less than 5 percent plant cover at these sites, and occasionally some are completely bare. The vegetation consists of small forbs, including trailing windmills and buckwheat, as well as six weeks three awn, an annual grass.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: The soils are small extent and are mapped in the lower Colorado Desert of California. MLRA: 40

SOIL SURVEY REGIONAL OFFICE (SSRO) RESPONSIBLE: Davis, California

SERIES ESTABLISHED: San Diego County, California, Anza-Borrego Area Soil Survey, 2012. The soil is named after Fonts Point, a popular trail and vantage point in Anza-Borrego Desert State Park.
