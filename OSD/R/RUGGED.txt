RUGGED SERIES

Depth class: moderately deep to paralithic bedrock
Drainage class: well drained
Parent material: loamy-skeletal colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock
Landscape: mountains
Landform: mountain slopes
Slope: 20 to 100 percent
Mean annual precipitation: about 2380 mm (94 in)
Mean annual air temperature: about 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.)
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy-skeletal, mixed, superactive Typic Haplocryepts

TYPICAL PEDON: Rugged extremely gravelly sandy loam in a subalpine alder-devil's club community on an east facing linear, linear backslope on a slope of 57 percent at an elevation of 274m (899ft).

     A1--0 to 18 cm (0 to 7 in); extremely gravelly sandy loam, very dark brown (10YR 2/2) moist; dark grayish brown (10YR 4/2) dry; weak fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many very fine and fine roots and common medium roots throughout; 5 percent stones by volume, 25 percent cobbles by volume, 55 percent gravels by volume; moderately acid (pH 5.9); clear smooth boundary.

     A2--18 to 47 cm (7 to 19 in); extremely gravelly sandy loam, brown (10YR 4/3) moist, pale brown (10YR 6/3) dry; weak fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many very fine and medium roots and common coarse roots throughout; 5 percent stones by volume, 20 percent cobbles by volume, 60 percent gravels by volume; moderately acid (pH 5.9), clear smooth boundary.

     2BC--47 to 58 cm (19 to 23 in); very gravelly fine sandy loam, brown (7.5YR 4/4) moist, light brown (7.5YR 6/4) dry; weak fine granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common very fine and fine roots throughout; 5 percent cobbles by volume, 50 percent gravels by volume; moderately acid (pH 5.9), abrupt smooth boundary.

     2Cr--58 to 83 cm (22.8 to 32.7 in); moderately coherent metasedimentary bedrock

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.1402778, longitude -149.4619444, datum WGS84.
