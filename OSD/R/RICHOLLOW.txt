RICHOLLOW SERIES

The Richollow series consists of shallow, well drained soils that formed in colluvium over residuum from limestone, dolomite, calcareous siltstone or calcareous sandstone. These soils are hillslopes, mountain slopes, and ridges. Slopes are 5 to 50[RC-NIFI1.1] percent. The mean annual precipitation is about 500 mm and the mean annual air temperature is about 3.3 degrees C. 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy-skeletal, mixed, superactive Lithic Calcicryolls

TYPICAL PEDON: Richollow very gravelly silt loam, extremely stony in rangeland. (Colors are for dry soil unless otherwise noted.) 

    A--0 to 18 cm; grayish brown (10YR 5/2) very gravelly silt loam, very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2) moist; moderate fine granular structure; soft, very friable, non-sticky and non-plastic; many very fine, fine and few medium roots; many fine irregular pores; strongly effervescent; 40 percent gravel and 5 percent cobbles; slightly alkaline (pH 7.7); gradual wavy boundary.

    Bk--18 to 33 cm; light gray (10YR 7/2) extremely cobbly silt loam, brown (10YR 5/3) moist; moderate fine subangular blocky structure; soft, very friable, non-sticky and non-plastic; many very fine, fine and few medium roots; common fine irregular pores; 25 percent fine and medium threadlike calcium carbonate masses, 25 percent fine and medium calcium carbonate masses; violently effervescent; 30 percent gravel and 40 percent cobbles; moderately alkaline (pH 8.0); abrupt wavy boundary.

    R--33 cm; limestone. 

TYPE LOCATION: Bear Lake County, Idaho; USGS Paris topographic quadrangle; latitude 42.2065833 degrees north, longitude 111.4495556 degrees west, datum WGS84.

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING:

Elevation--1,820 to 2,870 meters
Slope--5 to 50 percent
Parent material--colluvium over residuum from limestone, dolomite, calcareous siltstone or calcareous sandstone
Climate--long, cold winters; moist springs; cool, dry summers
Mean annual precipitation--410 to 910[RC-NIFI2.1] mm
Mean annual temperature--2.0 to 5.0 degrees C
Frost-free period--30 to 70 days

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: None listed.

DRAINAGE AND PERMEABILITY: Well drained, 15 to 50 um/sec.

USE AND VEGETATION: Richollow soils are used for grazing and wildlife habitat. The potential native vegetation is low sage, mountain big sage, bluebunch wheatgrass, Idaho fescue, arrowleaf balsamroot and twisted leaf.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: Richollow soils are of small extent in Southeastern Idaho; MLRA 43B, 47.

SOIL SURVEY REGIONAL OFFICE (SSRO) RESPONSIBLE: Bozeman, Montana.

SERIES ESTABLISHED: Bear Lake County, Idaho, 2008. The name is derived from nearby Rich Hollow. 

REMARKS: Diagnostic horizons and features recognized in this pedon are:
Mollic epipedon--0 to 18 cm (A horizon)
Calcic horizon--18 to 33 cm (Bk horizon)
Lithic contact--33 cm

Particle-size control section--0 to 33 cm

Richollow soils have a cryic temperature regime and a xeric moisture regime.
