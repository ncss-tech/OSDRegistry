YAQUIPASS SERIES

The Yaquipass series consists of very shallow and shallow, somewhat excessively drained soils that are formed in residuum from granite and gneiss. Yaquipass soils are on mountains with slopes 8 to 85 percent. The mean annual precipitation is 150 mm and the mean annual air temperature is about 21 degrees C.

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Sandy-skeletal, mixed, hyperthermic Lithic Torriorthents

TYPICAL PEDON: Yaquipass gravelly loamy sand on a slope of 31 percent, elevation of 525 meters, and cover of brittle bush, teddy bear cholla, creosote, agave, and ocotillo. Colors are for dry soil unless otherwise noted. Surface fragment cover is 23 percent gravel, 15 percent cobbles, and 2 percent stones.

  A--0 to 2 cm; pale brown (10YR 6/3) gravelly loamy sand, brown (10YR 4/3) moist; moderate thick platy structure; very friable, soft, slightly sticky, nonplastic; common very fine and fine roots; common very fine vesicular pores; slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); slight effervescence; abrupt smooth boundary. (2 to 38 cm thick)

  Bw--2 to 23 cm; yellowish brown (10YR 5/4) very gravelly loamy sand, dark yellowish brown (10YR 4/4) moist; weak medium subangular block structure; very friable, soft, slightly sticky, nonplastic; common very fine, fine, and medium roots; common irregular pores; slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); slight effervescence; clear wavy boundary.(8 to 27 cm thick)

  C--23 to 42 cm; pale brown (10YR 6/3) very gravelly coarse sand, brown (10YR 5/3) moist; structureless single grain; loose, nonsticky, nonplastic; common medium roots; many interstitial pores; slightly alkaline (pH 7.6); slight effervescence. (6 to 32 cm thick)

  R--42 to 200 cm; granite.

TYPE LOCATION: San Diego County, California; approximately 1.3 kilometers (0.8 miles) north and east of where of San Filipe Wash intersects the Hill of the Moons Wash; 33.161439 degrees latitude, -116.352869 degrees longitude WGS84; UTM Zone 11, 3669370 N, 560342E. USGS Quad Name: Borego Sink, California.

USE AND VEGETATION: This soil is used for recreation and wildlife habitat. Vegetation is dominantly brittlebush, creosote, agave, ocotillo, Gander's buckhorn cholla, teddybear cholla, silver cholla, Emory's rockdaisy, and barrel cactus.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: The soils are of small extent and are mapped in the lower Colorado Desert of California. MLRA40.
