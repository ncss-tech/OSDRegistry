BLYING SERIES

Depth class: very shallow to bedrock, lithic 
Drainage class: well drained 
Parent material: coarse-loamy colluvium over residuum weathered from metasedimentary rock 
Landscape: mountains 
Landform: mountain slopes 
Slope: 5 to 35 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (73 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C (37 degrees F) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Loamy, mixed, superactive Lithic Dystrocryepts

TYPICAL PEDON: Blying highly organic silt loam in a maritime stunted woodland shallow organic slopes community on a northeast facing convex, convex, mountain slope on a slope of 33 percent at an elevation of 255m (837 ft).

     Oe--0 to 5 cm (0 to 2 in); moderately decomposed plant material, black (7.5YR 2.5/1) moist, dark gray (7.5YR 4/1) dry; common medium and coarse roots and many fine; extremely acid (pH 4.4); clear wavy boundary.

     A--5 to 20 cm (2 to 8 in); highly organic silt loam, very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2) moist, brown (7.5YR 4/2) dry; moderate medium granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; many fine and medium roots and common coarse roots; extremely acid (pH 4.4); clear wavy boundary.

     C--20 to 25 cm (8 to 10 in); channery very fine sandy loam, gray (N 6/), moist, light gray (N 7/), dry; structureless massive; friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; common fine, medium, and coarse roots; 10 percent fine strong brown (7.5YR 4/6), moist, masses of oxidized iron; 30 percent angular channers by volume; very strongly acid (pH 4.8); clear broken boundary.

     R--25 to 50 cm (10 to 20 in); strongly coherent metasedimentary bedrock.

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska: latitude 60.1061111, longitude -149.3450000, datum WGS84
