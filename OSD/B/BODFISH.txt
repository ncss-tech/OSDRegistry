GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Arrakis, Atigaru, Kikak and Tuluga soils.
Anajuk soils are very poorly drained, have permafrost, have cryoturbated and glacic horizons, and occur on low relief troughs of high-center polygons.
Arrakis soils have a regular decrease in organic material by depth, eolian sands at parent material, and occur on the meander scrolls and active dunes.
Atigaru soils are poorly drained, have permafrost, and occur on non-polygonal stream terraces.
Kikak soils are poorly drained, have aquic conditions, have permafrost below 100 cm, and occur on flats of ice wedge polygons.
Tuluga soils are poorly drained, have aquic conditions, and occur on depressions on stream terraces.
