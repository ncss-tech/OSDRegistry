TYPICAL PEDON: Bansheecreek slightly decomposed plant material in a dwarf shrub and sedge community on patterned ground at an elevation of 45 m (150 ft)

	Oi1--0 to 8 centimeters (0 to 3 inches); dark reddish brown (5YR 3/3); slightly decomposed plant material; many very fine and fine, and common medium and coarse roots throughout; neutral, (pH 7.3); clear smooth boundary.

	Oi2--8 to 14 centimeters (3 to 6 inches); very dark brown (7.5YR 2.5/2); slightly decomposed plant material; many very and fine, and common medium roots throughout; neutral, (pH 6.8); abrupt wavy boundary. (9 to 39 cm, 4 to 15 in)

	Oa1--14 to 25 centimeters (6 to 10 inches); dark reddish brown (5YR 3/3); highly decomposed plant material; many very fine and fine roots throughout; moderately acid, (pH 6.0); abrupt smooth boundary.

	Oa2--25 to 29 centimeters (10 to 11 in); black (10YR 2/1); highly decomposed plant material; many very fine and fine roots throughout; moderately acid, (pH 5.8); clear smooth boundary. (10 to 22 cm, 4 to 9 in)

	Oaf--29 to 103 centimeters (11 to 41 in); very dark brown (10YR 2/2); permanently frozen highly decomposed plant material; moderately acid, (pH 5.7); clear smooth boundary. (52 to 98 cm, 20 to 39 in)

	Cgf--103 to 200 centimeters (41 to 79 in); dark gray (2.5Y 4/1); permanently frozen very fine sandy loam; nonsticky, nonplastic; nonsmeary; moderately acid, (pH 5.9).

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Anajuk, Inigok, Nuiqsut, Pingok, and Poleakoon soils.
Anajuk soils are very poorly drained, have a histic epipedon, and occur on high centered polygons on patterned ground.
Ingok soils are poorly drained, have a histic epipedon, have a glacic layer, have a cryoturbated horizon, and occur on low relief domes of high centered polygons.
Nuiqsut soils are poorly drained, have a mineral soil contact within 70 cm (28 in), and occur at the center of a low-center polygon.
Pingok soils are very poorly drained, have a glacic horizon, are dominated by hemic materials, and occur on high relief troughs of high-centered polygons.
Poleakoon soils are very poorly drained, have a histic epipedon, and occur along streams and in string bongs.
