BLACKRAIL SERIES

MLRA(s)--152A
Depth class--Very deep
Drainage class--Very poorly drained
Parent material--Sandy alluvial deposits
Geomorphic location--Oligohaline and mesohaline tidal marshes along tidal river floodplains
Slope range--0 to 1 percent
Mean annual air temperature--21 degrees C (70 degrees F)
Mean annual precipitation--1397 millimeters (55 inches)

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Siliceous, hyperthermic Humaqueptic Psammaquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Blackrail mucky fine sand in an oligohaline marsh 0.3 meters above sea level (colors are for moist soil unless otherwise stated; electrical conductivity is 1:1 by volume solution mixture unless otherwise stated).

 Aseg--0 to 31 centimeters; very dark grayish brown (10YR 3/2) mucky fine sand; single grain; nonsticky, nonplastic; nonfluid; many very fine and fine, and few medium roots; no sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; nonsaline (0.2 dS/m); neutral (pH 6.6), slightly acid (pH 6.2) oxidized pH; clear boundary

 Cseg1--31 to 52 centimeters; 80 percent black (2.5Y 2.5/1) and 20 percent light olive brown (2.5Y 5/3) loamy fine sand; single grain; nonsticky, nonplastic; nonfluid; common very fine and fine roots; slight sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; nonsaline (0.6 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.0), slightly acid (pH 6.5) oxidized pH; clear boundary

 Cseg2--52 to 200 centimeters; very dark gray (2.5Y 3/1) loamy fine sand; single grain; nonsticky, nonplastic; nonfluid; few fine roots; moderate sulfurous odor; no color change with 3 percent hydrogen peroxide; slight effervescence with 30 percent hydrogen peroxide; very slightly saline (3.2 dS/m); neutral (pH 7.1), slightly acid (pH 6.3) oxidized pH

TYPE LOCATION: Franklin County, Florida; Crooked River; approximately 1.6 kilometers (1.0 mile) west of the intersection of County Road 67 and Kendrick Road, and approximately 1.4 kilometers (0.9 miles) north of the intersection of Georgia Avenue and Wellborn Avenue; USGS topographic quadrangle: Pickett Bay.

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS:
These are the Fushatchee, Haulover, Millender, and Yent soils.
Fushatchee soils--Formed in herbaceous organic material over loamy estuarine deposits and have over 40 centimeters of hemic material at the soil surface.
Haulover soils--Formed in fine-loamy estuarine deposits and contain sulfidic materials within the top 50 centimeters of the soil profile. 
Millender soils--Formed in fine-loamy estuarine deposits and contain sulfidic materials between 50 and 100 centimeters from the soil surface. 
Yent soils--Formed in herbaceous and woody organic material over loamy estuarine deposits and have over 40 centimeters of sapric material at the surface. 

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY:
Drainage class--Very poorly drained
Saturated hydraulic conductivity--High to very high
Soil moisture regime--Peraquic
Flooding--Very frequent, for brief durations. These soils are flooded by wind and lunar tides, or overbank flow. Inundation depths on Blackrail soils are irregular. 

USE AND VEGETATION:
Major uses--Blackrail soils are used mainly as wetland wildlife habitat.
Typical vegetation--Blackrail soils are dominated by black needlerush (Juncus roemerianus), with interspersed sawgrass (Cladium jamaicense), and cosmopolitan bullrush (Bolboschoenus maritimus). Along areas adjacent to major floodplains, stands of slash pine (Pinus elliottii) and cabbage palm (Sabal palmetto) establish.

REMARKS:
The series is named after the black rail bird (Laterallus jamaicensis) who utilize areas of this soil for habitat and foraging. Blackrail soils were previously mapped as the Bayvi, Bohicket, Dirego, and Tisonia soils in Florida soil surveys.

Diagnostic horizons and other diagnostic soil characteristics recognized in this pedon are (Keys to Soil Taxonomy, Thirteenth Edition, 2022):
Ochric epipedon--The zone from 0 to 31 centimeters.
Peraquic feature--Zero or positive water potential at or near the soil surface.
