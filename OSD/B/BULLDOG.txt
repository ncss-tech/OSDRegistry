BULLDOG SERIES

Depth class: very deep 
Drainage class: poorly drained 
Parent material: coarse-loamy alluvium over sandy alluvium derived from metasedimentary rock and/or sandy and gravelly alluvium derived from metasedimentary rock 
Landscape: shore complexes 
Landform: beach plains 
Slope: 0 to 2 percent 
Mean annual precipitation: about 1870 mm (74 in) 
Mean annual air temperature: about 3 degrees C. (37 degrees F.) 
Frost-free period: 130 to 155 days 

TAXONOMIC CLASS: Coarse-loamy over sandy or sandy-skeletal, mixed, superactive, nonacid Typic Cryaquents

TYPICAL PEDON: Bulldog silt in a dwarf willow and graminoid community on a south facing linear, linear slope on a slope of 1 percent at an elevation of 3 m (10 ft).

     Oi--0 to 2 cm (0 to 1 in); slightly decomposed plant material, black (10YR 2/1) moist and dark gray (10YR 4/1) dry; many very fine and fine roots throughout; medium vesicular pores; moderately acid (pH 5.9); clear smooth boundary.

     A--2 to 7 cm (1 to 3 in); silt, very dark gray (10YR 3/1) moist and gray (10YR 5/1) dry; weak medium granular structure; very friable, nonsticky, moderately plastic; many very fine and fine roots throughout; fine vesicular pores; 30 percent medium distinct irregular iron-manganese masses; slightly acid (pH 6.1); clear smooth boundary.

     Cg1--7 to 80 cm (3 to 32 in); silt loam, very dark bluish gray (5B 3/1) moist and bluish gray (5B 5/1) dry; weak medium platy structure; firm, slightly sticky, slightly plastic; many very fine and fine roots throughout; fine vesicular pores; 40 percent coarse distinct irregular iron-manganese masses; moderately acid (pH 5.9); clear wavy boundary.

     Cg2--80 to 200 cm (32 to 79 in); loamy sand, bluish black (5PB 2.5/1) moist and dark bluish gray (5PB 4/1) dry; massive; very friable, nonsticky, nonplastic; interstitial pores; 10 percent coarse distinct irregular iron-manganese masses; strongly acid (pH 5.4).

TYPE LOCATION: Kenai Peninsula Borough, Alaska; latitude 60.1264767, longitude -149.3860366, datum WGS84

COMPETING SERIES: These are the Niklavar (AK) and Swedna (AK) series. 
Swedna soils lack silt loam and silt textures in the A horizon, have mean annual air temperature less than 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F.), and have frost-free periods greater than 50 days. 
Niklavar soils have reaction classes below moderately acid and have a mean annual air temperature of less then 2 degrees C. (36 degrees F).

GEOGRAPHIC SETTING: 
Elevation: 0 to 10 meters (0 to 33 feet) 
Parent material: coarse-loamy alluvium over sandy alluvium derived from metasedimentary rock and/or sandy and gravelly alluvium derived from metasedimentary rock 
Landform: beach plains

Frost free period:130 to 155 days 

GEOGRAPHICALLY ASSOCIATED SOILS: These are the Resurrection soils. 
Resurrection soils flood occasionally, are sandy skeletal throughout, lack redoximorphic features and occur on berms in shore complexes. 

DRAINAGE AND SATURATED HYDRAULIC CONDUCTIVITY: 
Drainage class: poorly drained 
Saturated Hydraulic Conductivity: moderately high to high in the A and Cg horizons, and very high in the Oi and C horizons. 
Permeability: moderately rapid to very rapid in the A and Cg horizons, and rapid to very rapid in the Oi and C horizons. 
Runoff: very high 
Flooding: frequent and long 

USE AND VEGETATION: 
Use: wildlife habitat, recreation, and subsistence.
Native vegetation: silverweed cinquefoil, sea milkwort, tufted hairgrass, common dandelion, fringed willowherb, American dunegrass, foxtail barley, alkali buttercup, beach pea, saltmarsh starwort, fowl bluegrass, rock sedge, Alaska bog willow, bluejoint grass, purple marshlocks, mountain rush.

DISTRIBUTION AND EXTENT: MLRA 220, Alexander Archipelago-Gulf of Alaska Coast. The series is of small extent.
